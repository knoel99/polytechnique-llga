The recommended track — "balanced profile", Graph + LLM core

Four tracks are possible (Graph AI / Structured Generative AI · LLM Engineering & Agentic Systems · Hybrid Neuro-Symbolic / GraphRAG · Research/PhD). The heart of the program is the LLM + graphs intersection, hence the selection written up here:

  • P1: the 2 refresher courses + Machine Learning (core curriculum), then at the choice point: Deep Learning + Signal Processing + Emerging Subjects in ML;
  • P2: RL & Autonomous Agents, Graph ML & DL for Generative AI (central course) and Text Mining & NLP (core curriculum), then at the choice point: Computational Optimal Transport and Multimodal Generative AI;
  • Year 2: Large Language Models, Advanced Graph Neural Networks, Analysis & Deep Learning on Geometric Data.

Year 1 — Foundations (P1) · core of the program (P2) · research internship (P3) — all MSc&T specializations: sports, humanities and languages as transverse courses

PERIOD 1 — Foundations — refresher courses, machine learning, first scientific options

2 refresher courses (Core curriculum)

  • Refresher in Statistics APM_51438_EP · Marine Le Morvan (Inria) — Refresher · 36 h Probability, random variables, estimation, tests, regression — the statistical foundations required by every module of the program.
  • Refresher in Computer Science CSC_51440_EP · Amal Dev Parakkat (Télécom Paris) — Refresher · 36 h Advanced Python, data structures, complexity, graph algorithms — the computing toolkit of the program.

Mandatory courses (Core curriculum)

  • Machine Learning MDC_51006_EP · Erwan Le Pennec & Jesse Read (École Polytechnique) — Mandatory · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS Supervised and unsupervised learning: regression, regularization, SVMs, trees and boosting, k-means, PCA, EM, kernels, generalization theory.
  • Computer Animation CSC_51085_EP · Mathieu Desbrun & Marie-Paule Cani (Inria & École Polytechnique) — Mandatory (brochure) · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS Physical simulation, integrators (stability proved), rigid bodies, collisions, skinning, crowd animation.

Select 2 courses (curriculum: 3 choices)

The brochure requires 2 courses from its four options; the detailed LLGA curriculum enlarges the group and requires 3 courses (including at least one Deep Learning course during the year). The links are the recommended track's choices.

  • Deep Learning CSC_51054_EP · Michalis Vazirgiannis, Johannes Lutzeyer & Ye Zhu (École Polytechnique) — Recommended — track · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS From perceptrons to transformers: backpropagation, MLPs, CNNs, RNNs, attention, autoencoders, GANs and training in practice (PyTorch).
  • Signal Processing APM_51055_EP · Rémi Flamary (École Polytechnique, co-director of the program) — Recommended — track · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS Signals and systems, sampling, Fourier, convolution and filtering, time-frequency — with a bridge to ML and optimal transport.
  • Emerging Subjects in ML & Collaborative Learning APM_51178_EP · Aymeric Dieuleveut & El Mahdi El Mhamdi (École Polytechnique) — Recommended — track · detailed curriculum Federated and distributed learning, distributed SGD, Byzantine robustness, differential privacy, collaboration and multi-agent systems.
  • Digital Representation and Analysis of Shapes CSC_51074_EP · Mathieu Desbrun & Pooran Memari (École Polytechnique & Inria) — P1 option · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS Meshes, Voronoi/Delaunay (empty circle criterion proved), remeshing, QEM simplification, curvature, shape spectrum.
  • Topological Data Analysis CSC_51056_EP · Steve Oudot (École Polytechnique & Inria) — P1 option · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS Simplicial complexes, persistent homology (algorithm proved step by step), stability, Mapper, TDA × ML.
  • Image Analysis and Computer Vision CSC_51073_EP · Mathieu Brédif (Univ. Gustave Eiffel) — P1 option · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS Image formation, filtering, SIFT, projective geometry (homographies proved), Lucas-Kanade, ICP and 3D point clouds.
  • Probability Theory for ML: Monte Carlo and Generative Models APM_51056_EP · Alain Durmus (École Polytechnique) — P1 option · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS Advanced probability, simulation, importance sampling, MCMC (Metropolis-Hastings, Gibbs), Langevin and the link with diffusion.
  • DataBase Management System CSC_51053_EP · Ioana Manolescu (Inria & École Polytechnique) — P1 option · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS Relational model, SQL, transactions (2PL proved), B+ tree indexes, optimization, NoSQL and vector databases (HNSW).
  • Statistical Learning Theory APM_51059_EP · Karim Lounici (École Polytechnique) — P1 option · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS Concentration (Hoeffding proved), VC dimension (Sauer proved), Rademacher, minimax, Lasso and aggregation.

PERIOD 2 — The heart of the program — RL, graphs, NLP, optimal transport

Mandatory courses (Core curriculum)

  • Reinforcement Learning and Autonomous Agents CSC_52081_EP · Jesse Read (École Polytechnique) · "Advanced Machine Learning and Autonomous Agents" in the brochure — Mandatory · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS MDPs, Bellman, dynamic programming, TD-learning, Q-learning, policy gradients, actor-critic, DQN, and modern autonomous agents.
  • Graph Machine and Deep Learning for Generative AI CSC_52072_EP · Johannes Lutzeyer & Michalis Vazirgiannis (École Polytechnique) — Mandatory · Central course · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS The central course of the program: graph theory, node embeddings, GNNs and message passing, graph generation, GraphRAG.
  • Introduction to Text Mining and NLP CSC_52082_EP · Michalis Vazirgiannis & Davide Buscaldi (École Polytechnique / USPN) — Mandatory · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS From raw text to transformers: tokenization, TF-IDF, embeddings, topic models, classification, NER, HuggingFace.
  • Computer Vision: From Fundamentals to Applications Brochure MSc&T · Mandatory according to the brochure (period 2) — Mandatory (brochure) · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS Detection, segmentation, tracking, 3D and deployment — the vision pipeline oriented toward industrial applications.

  • Real-time AI in Video Games: decisive & collaborative actions Brochure MSc&T · Brochure option — P2 option · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS Real-time AI: navigation (A*), behavior trees, MCTS, self-play, conversational NPCs.

  • Deep Learning (P2 variant) APM_52183_EP · Kevin Scaman (École Polytechnique) · efficiency and cost angle — P2 option · detailed curriculum FLOP/memory counting, data/tensor/pipeline parallelism, all-reduce, ZeRO, quantization, full budget of a 7B LLM.
  • Optimization for AI APM_52067_EP · Luiz Chamon & Aymeric Dieuleveut (École Polytechnique) — P2 option · 36 h · 4.5 ECTS Convexity and duality, gradient descent (full proofs), Nesterov, proximal methods, SGD, SAGA/SVRG, hyperparameters.

Year 2 — LLMs & Graph AI specialization (P1 · P2) · seminar · transverse learning · end-of-studies internship

PERIOD 1 — Specialization — LLMs, Graph AI, geometry (courses offered in the M2 brochure)

Courses offered

  • Large Language Models CSC_53432_EP · Guokan Shang (MBZUAI) — M2 · 24 h · 2 ECTS Transformers in depth, pretraining, post-training (SFT, RLHF, DPO, GRPO), RAG, agents, multimodality.
  • Advanced Graph Neural Networks M2 · Graph AI · LLGA team — M2 · 24 h Expressiveness and Weisfeiler-Lehman, over-squashing, positional encodings, graph transformers, temporal GNNs, scale.
  • Analysis and Deep Learning on Geometric Data CSC_53431_EP · Maks Ovsjanikov (École Polytechnique) — M2 · 24 h · 2 ECTS Discrete differential geometry, Laplace-Beltrami, functional maps, DiffusionNet, shape correspondence.
  • Privacy and Uncertainty Quantification M2 · P1 (brochure) · Period 1 — M2 · ~24 h Differential privacy (composition, Rényi DP) and uncertainty quantification: calibration, ensembles, conformal prediction.
  • From Boosting to Foundation Models: learning with Tabular Data M2 · P1 (brochure) · Period 1 — M2 · ~24 h Why GBDTs dominate, XGBoost/LightGBM/CatBoost from the inside, deep tabular, TabPFN, LLMs and tabular data.
  • Control in Generative AI M2 · P1 (brochure) · Period 1 — M2 · ~24 h Constrained decoding (regex→automata), steering vectors, watermarking (z-test proved), control of diffusion models and graph generators.

PERIOD 2 — Specialization — engineering, safety, generation (courses offered in the M2 brochure)

Courses offered

  • ML Ops and LLM Engineering Principles M2 · P2 (brochure) · Period 2 — M2 · ~24 h Industrialization: ML CI/CD, serving (vLLM, continuous batching), drift and monitoring, RAG ops, costs, green ops.
  • Fundamentals of Security and Robustness for AI M2 · P2 (brochure) · Period 2 — M2 · ~24 h Adversarial attacks (FGSM/PGD with proofs), defenses and certification, poisoning, model stealing, LLM/GNN robustness.
  • Graph Generative Models and Applications M2 · P2 (brochure) · Period 2 · M2 extension of the M1 central course — M2 · ~24 h Graph generation: graphons, autoregressive models, VAEs, flows, diffusion (DiGress), evaluation, molecules and LLMs.
  • Explainability, Security, Privacy of LLMs M2 · P2 (brochure) · Period 2 — M2 · ~24 h Probing, SAEs and superposition, jailbreaks and injections, memorization, DP for LLMs, watermarking, red teaming.
  • Speech Technology for Conversational AI M2 · P2 (brochure) · Period 2 — M2 · ~24 h ASR (CTC fully derived, Whisper, streaming), TTS and vocoders, cloning and ethics, full-duplex conversation, speech LLMs.
  • Advanced Topics in Large Language Models M2 · P2 (brochure) · Period 2 · research seminar — M2 · ~24 h Reasoning (RLVR), long context, memory, MoE, alternative architectures (Mamba), LLMs × graphs, agents, open problems.
