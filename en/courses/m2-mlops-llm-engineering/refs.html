References

  • D. Sculley, G. Holt, D. Golovin, et al., Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems, NeurIPS 2015 — the founding act; the rest of the module unrolls its debt.
  • G.-I. Yu, J. S. Jeong, G.-W. Kim, S. Kim, B.-G. Chun, Orca: A Distributed Serving System for Transformer-Based Generative Models, OSDI 2022 — continuous batching (iteration-level scheduling); W. Kwon, Z. Li, S. Zhuang, et al., Efficient Memory Management for Large Language Model Serving with PagedAttention (vLLM), SOSP 2023 — paged KV, shared prefixes.
  • S. Es, J. James, L. Espinosa-Anke, S. Schockaert, RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation, EACL 2024 (demo) — faithfulness, answer relevancy, context precision/recall.
  • E. J. Hu, Y. Shen, P. Wallis, et al., LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models, ICLR 2022 — the foundation of ch. 7 (reminder of the LLMs module ch. 4).
  • D. Edge, H. Trinh, N. Cheng, et al., From Local to Global: A Graph RAG Approach to Query-Focused Summarization, 2024 — the graph option of ch. 6 (cf. Graph ML module ch. 11).
  • L. Zheng, W.-T. Chiang, Y. Sheng, et al., Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena, NeurIPS 2023 — agreement with humans, position/length/self bias.
  • J. Gama, I. Žliobaitė, A. Bifet, M. Pechenizkiy, A. Bouchachia, A Survey on Concept Drift Adaptation, ACM Computing Surveys 2014 — the vocabulary of ch. 5.
  • D. Patterson, J. Gonzalez, Q. Le, et al., Carbon Emissions and Large Neural Network Training, 2021; S. Luccioni, A. Hernandez-Garcia, A. Voznyuk, et al., Estimating the Carbon Footprint of BLOOM, 2022 — the carbon accounting of ch. 10.
  • B. Beyer, C. Jones, J. Petoff, N. Murphy, Site Reliability Engineering, O'Reilly 2016 — SLOs, error budgets, post-mortems; H. Huyen, Designing Machine Learning Systems, O'Reilly 2022 — the ML Ops reference in book form.
  • Tooling: DVC (versioned data), MLflow (registry), Feast (feature store), Pandera and Great Expectations (data contracts), OpenTelemetry / Langfuse (tracing), CodeCarbon (energy), vLLM (serving).
  • Standards: OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications; NIST AI Risk Management Framework (Govern/Map/Measure/Manage) — the backbone of chapters 8-9, extended by the Explainability, Security, Privacy module.
