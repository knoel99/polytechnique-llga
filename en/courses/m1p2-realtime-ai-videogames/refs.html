References

  • S. Rabin (ed.), Game AI Pro (vol. 1–7) — the freely accessible chapters: behavior trees, utility, navigation, AI LOD.
  • M. Colledanchise, P. Ögren, Behavior Trees in Robotics and AI: An Introduction, CRC/Springer, 2018 — the formalization.
  • D. Isla, "Handling Complexity in the Halo 2 AI", GDC 2005 — the introduction of behavior trees in industry.
  • J. Orkin, "Three States and a Plan: The AI of F.E.A.R.", GDC 2006 — GOAP.
  • C. W. Reynolds, "Flocks, Herds and Schools: A Distributed Behavioral Model", SIGGRAPH 1987; "Steering Behaviors for Autonomous Characters", GDC 1999 — boids and steering.
  • P. E. Hart, N. J. Nilsson, B. Raphael, "A Formal Basis for the Heuristic Determination of Minimum Cost Paths", IEEE TSSC 4(2), 1968 — A*.
  • A. Botea, M. Müller, J. Schaeffer, "Near-Optimal Hierarchical Path-Finding (HPA*)", Journal of Game Development 1(1), 2004.
  • Recast/Detour — the standard open-source navmesh generation (M. Mononen).
  • R. Coulom, "Efficient Selectivity and Backup Operators in Monte-Carlo Tree Search", CIG 2006; L. Kocsis, C. Szepesvári, "Bandit Based Monte-Carlo Planning", ECML 2006 — MCTS and UCT.
  • C. Browne et al., A Survey of Monte Carlo Tree Search Methods, IEEE TCIAIG, 2012.
  • P. Auer, N. Cesa-Bianchi, P. Fischer, "Finite-time Analysis of the Multiarmed Bandit Problem", Machine Learning 47, 2002 — UCB1 (see RL module, chapter 6).
  • G. Tesauro, "TD-Gammon, a Self-Teaching Backgammon Program", 1992 — the self-play forerunner.
  • D. Silver et al., "Mastering the Game of Go without Human Knowledge" (AlphaZero), Nature 550, 2017; "Mastering the Game of Go with Deep Neural Networks and Tree Search", Nature 529, 2016.
  • O. Vinyals et al., "Grandmaster Level in StarCraft II Using Multi-agent Reinforcement Learning" (AlphaStar), Nature 575, 2019.
  • C. Berner et al., Dota 2 with Large Scale Deep Reinforcement Learning (OpenAI Five), 2019.
  • B. Baker et al., Emergent Tool Use in Multi-Agent Interaction (hide-and-seek), NeurIPS 2019.
  • J. S. Park et al., Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior, UIST 2023.
  • G. Wang et al., Voyager: An Open-Ended Embodied Agent with Large Language Models, 2023 — LLM agents in open worlds (see RL module, chapter 7, and the M2 LLMs module).
  • T. Ford et al., "Overwatch Gameplay Architecture and Netcode", GDC 2017 — the ECS architecture in production.
  • J. Nystrom, Game Programming Patterns — free online (game loop, object pools, components).
  • R. Smith, "Atomic Habits of Lockstep" / documentation of RTS lockstep mode — determinism and networking (see chapter 9.3 for the synthesis).
  • Instructor's note: an MSc&T brochure option (period 2); the referenced instructor and the lab engine (Godot/Unity/Python) will be confirmed at the start of the period — the references above cover the standard game AI core.
