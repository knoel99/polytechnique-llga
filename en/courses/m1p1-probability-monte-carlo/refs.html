References

  • A. Durmus, É. Moulines, Non-asymptotic convergence analysis for the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm, Annals of Applied Probability 27(3), 2017 — Theorem 9.5 and chapter 10 [1].
  • A. Durmus, É. Moulines, High-dimensional Bayesian inference via the Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm, Annals of Statistics 45(4), 2017 — dimension, EMA, empirical averages: chapter 10 [2].
  • N. Brosse, A. Durmus, É. Moulines, S. Sabanis, The Tamed Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm, Stochastic Processes and their Applications 129(10), 2019 — chapter 10 [3].
  • C. Robert, G. Casella, Monte Carlo Statistical Methods, 2nd ed., Springer, 2004 — the pedagogical reference for chapters 5-8 (rejection, IS, MH, Gibbs, diagnostics).
  • D. Levin, Y. Peres, E. Wilmer, Markov Chains and Mixing Times, AMS, 2009 — free online version: complete proofs of Theorem 4.3, mixing times, spectrum (Example 4.4).
  • A. Gelman, J. B. Carlin, H. S. Stern, D. B. Dunson, A. Vehtari, D. B. Rubin, Bayesian Data Analysis, 3rd ed., CRC Press, 2013 — book website and data: \(\hat R\) diagnostics, ESS, MCMC practice of ch. 8.
  • J. R. Norris, Markov Chains, Cambridge University Press, 1997 — free pdf version: general theory of chains (ch. 4), reversibility, ergodic theorem.
  • P. Billingsley, Probability and Measure, 3rd ed., Wiley, 1995 — chapters 1-3: Lebesgue construction, Tonelli-Fubini, characteristic functions, proof of the CLT (Thm 2.3), conditional expectation.
  • J. S. Liu, Monte Carlo Strategies in Scientific Computing, Springer, 2001 — importance sampling, weights and degeneracy, sequential methods (ch. 7).
  • A. E. Gelfand, A. F. M. Smith, "Sampling-Based Approaches to Calculating Marginal Densities", JASA 85(410), 1990 — the paper that launched the modern Gibbs sampler (Prop. 8.3).
  • J. Ho, A. Jain, P. Abbeel, Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models, NeurIPS 2020 — and Y. Song et al., Score-Based Generative Modeling through Stochastic Differential Equations, ICLR 2021 — the "generative models" continuation of Prop. 9.6 (see the Multimodal GenAI module, chapter 3).
