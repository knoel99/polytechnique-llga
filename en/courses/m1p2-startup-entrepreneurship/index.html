Stakes (Extrapolating)

Extrapolated notes. Brochure title: Startup and Large Companies: Building a Bridge for Innovation or Entrepreneurship for Sustainability / Case Studies on Innovation. Shared thread: how an innovation (often technical) crosses the startup ↔ large-company boundary under governance, risk, and sometimes sustainability constraints.

After P1 strategy/marketing, P2 pushes to cases: alliances, scaling, and friction between startup speed and corporate process — very visible when industrializing an AI model.

Place in the 2-year path

Upstream: Strategy / Marketing. Here (P2): applied innovation during the RL / Graph ML / NLP core. Downstream: P3 research internship, then M2 transverse project + final internship.

Industry and research links

Industry: intrapreneurship, corporate venture, tech transfer, AI product ops. Research: result maturity (TRL), IP, open-source vs proprietary — recurring in lab internships and CIFRE.

How to approach it

  • Take a P2 technical deliverable (e.g. graph+text demo) and write the startup/corporate “bridge”: who pays, who operates, what risk.
  • If the sustainability option is chosen, cross-link with responsible / optimization modules.
