8. Research of the teaching team

Team update (last official sheet, 2025-2026): the CSC_52073_EP course unit “Optimisation and Responsible AI” has two coordinator-instructors, Hossein Nick Zinat Matin and Sonia Vanier (occurrence coordinator: S. Vanier). The course unit is not carried over into the 2026-2027 catalog; in 2026-2027, an identical title appears under the code CSC_51076_EP (period 1). The works presented below are those of Sonia Vanier's ORAILIX team.

Sonia Vanier (LIX, École Polytechnique) — operations research and decision support

Sonia Vanier is a professor at École Polytechnique (LIX, CNRS, Institut Polytechnique de Paris), where she leads the ORAILIX team (founded in 2024): a crossing of operations research and machine learning for industrial decision support, including an "Optimization for Mobility" chair — exactly the territory of chapters 4 and 5 of this module. Verified representative publications:

  • C. Gicquel, S. Vanier et al., Optimal deployment of virtual network functions for securing telecommunication networks against distributed denial of service attacks: a robust optimization approach, Computers & Operations Research, 2022 — optimal placement of network functions under attack: ILP + robust optimization (uncertainty — here the attack volume — enters the model, like the uncertainty on \(c_t\) in ch. 5).
  • PhD theses supervised at LIX at the ML × OR interface — e.g. G. Iommazzo, "Algorithmic configuration by learning and optimization" (2021): automatically configuring solvers by learning their parameters — ch. 9 of the Optimization for AI module applied... to the tools of ch. 4.

Bibliographic honesty (as in ch. 7): the complete list of her publications, notably the most recent ones on the ML–optimization bridges within ORAILIX, is to be confirmed on her page (orailix.com) and on her DBLP profile ("Sonia Haddad-Vanier"); the elements above were verified at the time of writing.
