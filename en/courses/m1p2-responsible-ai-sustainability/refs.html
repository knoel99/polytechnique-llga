References

  • S. Boyd, L. Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, Cambridge Univ. Press, 2004 — ch. 4 (multi-objective, scalarization).
  • K. Miettinen, Nonlinear Multiobjective Optimization, Kluwer, 1999 — the Pareto/ε-constraints/Chebyshev reference.
  • L. A. Wolsey, Integer Programming, 2nd ed., Wiley, 2020 — ILP, formulations, branch and bound.
  • A. Radovanovic et al. (Google), Carbon-Aware Computing for Datacenters, IEEE Trans. Power Systems, 2022 — the industrial deployment of ch. 2 and 5.
  • E. Strubell, A. Ganesh, A. McCallum, Energy and Policy Considerations for Deep Learning in NLP, ACL 2019 — the paper that launched the debate.
  • D. Patterson et al., Carbon Emissions and Large Neural Network Training, 2021 — measurement, PUE, regions (the methodological corrective).
  • A. Lacoste et al., Quantifying the Carbon Emissions of Machine Learning, 2019 — the ML CO2 Impact tool.
  • P. Henderson et al., Towards the Systematic Reporting of the Energy and Carbon Footprints of Machine Learning, NeurIPS 2020 — the protocol of ch. 6.
  • R. Schwartz et al., Green AI, Communications of the ACM, 2020 — efficiency as a research metric.
  • G. Hinton, O. Vinyals, J. Dean, Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network, NeurIPS 2014 (workshop), 2015.
  • S. Han, H. Mao, W. J. Dally, Deep Compression: Compressing Deep Neural Networks with Pruning, Trained Quantization and Huffman Coding, ICLR 2016.
  • B. Jacob et al., Quantization and Training of Neural Networks for Efficient Integer-Arithmetic-Only Inference, CVPR 2018.
  • CodeCarbon and Electricity Maps — measurement and hourly intensity data (ch. 6).
  • PuLP / HiGHS — the TME solvers (ch. 4).
  • Teaching team (LIX, École Polytechnique): C. Gicquel, S. Vanier et al., Optimal deployment of virtual network functions for securing telecommunication networks against distributed denial of service attacks: a robust optimization approach, Computers & Operations Research, 2022; ORAILIX team (LIX) — operations research × learning, "Optimization for Mobility" chair.
