References

  • E. F. Codd, "A Relational Model of Data for Large Shared Data Banks", Communications of the ACM 13(6), 1970 — the founding paper of the relational model.
  • S. Abiteboul, R. Hull, V. Vianu, Foundations of Databases, Addison-Wesley, 1995 — the theoretical reference: algebra/calculus (Thm 1.7), dependencies, chase, normal forms.
  • A. Silberschatz, H. Korth, S. Sudarshan, Database System Concepts, 7th ed., McGraw-Hill, 2019 — the course textbook: storage, B+ trees, transactions, ARIES, distributed.
  • H. Garcia-Molina, J. Ullman, J. Widom, Database Systems: The Complete Book, 2nd ed., Prentice Hall, 2008 — ch. 13-14 (B+), 17-18 (concurrency), 15-16 (recovery).
  • D. Comer, "The Ubiquitous B-Tree", ACM Computing Surveys 11(2), 1979 — the classic survey on B-trees.
  • G. Graefe, "Query Evaluation Techniques for Large Databases", ACM Computing Surveys 25(2), 1993 — joins, costs, optimization, the source of ch. 8.
  • P. Bernstein, V. Hadzilacos, N. Goodman, Concurrency Control and Recovery in Database Systems, Addison-Wesley, 1987 — the theorems of ch. 6 (2PL, serializability), freely consultable online.
  • C. Mohan, D. Haderle, B. Lindsay, H. Pirahesh, P. Schwarz, "ARIES: A Transaction Recovery Method Supporting Fine-Granularity Locking and Roll-Backs Using Write-Ahead Logging", ACM TODS 17(1), 1992 — the paper of ch. 7.
  • S. Gilbert, N. Lynch, "Brewer's Conjecture and the Feasibility of Consistent, Available, Partition-Tolerant Web Services", SIGACT News 33(2), 2002 — the formalization of CAP (Thm 9.1).
  • N. Francis et al., "Cypher: An Evolving Query Language for Property Graphs", SIGMOD 2018 — the language of property graphs; on the RDF side: W3C, RDF 1.1 Concepts and SPARQL 1.1.
  • Y. Malkov, D. Yashunin, "Efficient and Robust Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search Using Hierarchical Navigable Small World Graphs", IEEE TPAMI 2018 — arXiv 1603.09320; H. Jégou, M. Douze, C. Schmid, "Product Quantization for Nearest Neighbor Search", IEEE TPAMI 33(1), 2011 — the two pillars of ch. 9.
  • J. Johnson, M. Douze, H. Jégou, "Billion-scale Similarity Search with GPUs", IEEE Trans. Big Data 2017 — arXiv 1702.08734: the FAISS library, the standard tool for vector labs.
  • S. Abiteboul, I. Manolescu, P. Rigaux, M.-C. Rousset, P. Senellart, Web Data Management, Cambridge University Press, 2011 — free online edition; T. Kaoudi, I. Manolescu, M. Zampetakis, Cloud-Based RDF Data Management, Morgan & Claypool, 2020.
  • T. Chanial et al., "ConnectionLens: Finding Connections across Heterogeneous Data Sources", PVLDB 11(12), 2018; A. Anadiotis et al., "Graph integration of structured, semistructured and unstructured data for data journalism", Information Systems 104, 2022 — the CEDAR team in action (ch. 10-11); complete publication list on dblp.
  • Documentation: SQLite window functions, MVCC in PostgreSQL, MongoDB manual, pgvector.
