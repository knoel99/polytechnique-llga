11. Research of the teaching team

The module is led by Madhulika Mohanty, Inria researcher (chargée de recherche) in the CEDAR team (Inria Saclay — École Polytechnique), founded and led by Ioana Manolescu, Inria senior researcher (directrice de recherche), a specialist in complex and heterogeneous data management. The team's work draws the arc of this module: from the foundations of data systems to the web of data, then to the use of data to verify information — Madhulika Mohanty contributes in particular on the querying and exploration of graph data. Verified selection:

  • Foundations and teaching of the field. S. Abiteboul, I. Manolescu, P. Rigaux, M.-C. Rousset, P. Senellart, Web Data Management, Cambridge University Press, 2011 — the reference treatise on web data management, freely available online. With T. Kaoudi and M. Zampetakis, she also published Cloud-Based RDF Data Management (Morgan & Claypool, 2020) — the RDF of ch. 9 seen from the cloud and distribution side.
  • RDF graph summaries. Real knowledge graphs are too large to be "seen" at a glance: G. Goasdoué, M. Guzewicz, I. Manolescu, "RDF Graph Summarization for First-Sight Structure Discovery", VLDB Journal 29(5), 2020 — building quotient summaries that approximate the graph in small fractions of its size, with a guarantee of exactness for "typical" query answers.
  • Heterogeneous integration: ConnectionLens. The ConnectionLens platform (demo PVLDB 11(12), 2018, with C. Chanial, M. Dziri, M. Galhardas, J. Leblay, M. Le Nguyen) integrates relational databases, JSON, CSV, XML trees, and texts into a single graph; the long version (Anadiotis, Balalau, Bouganim, Chimienti, Galhardas, Haddad, Horel, Manolescu, Youssef, Graph integration of structured, semistructured and unstructured data for data journalism, Information Systems 104, 2022) formalizes the model: the direct follow-up of ch. 10. A spectacular application: the automated detection of scientific conflicts of interest (CIKM 2021) by crossing author statements, patents, clinical trials, and funding.
  • Fact-checking and data journalism. Starting with "Computational fact-checking: a content management perspective" (S. Cazalens, J. Leblay, I. Manolescu, X. Lamarre, T. Tannier, PVLDB 11(12), 2018), the team treats claim verification as a data management problem: finding the sources, aligning them, evaluating the claims. The line continued with the verification of numbers (Balalau et al., TTO/TLS 2022, StatCheck demonstrator) then with the generation of factual counter-arguments; the ANR/DGA SourceSay project (with the Décodeurs of Le Monde) equips journalists to find the first source of a digital rumor.
  • AI × data. CEDAR's current program connects information extraction (LLMs and graphs: FactSpotter, Findings of EMNLP 2023), heterogeneous data querying, and trust — the meeting of ch. 10 with the NLP and LLMs modules of the master's program. See the CEDAR page and the dblp list.
Note — What this changes for you Project topics draw on this work: summarizing an RDF graph, integrating two heterogeneous sources with provenance, verifying a published statistic against open datasets — modeling (ch. 3), querying efficiently (ch. 5-8), assessing trust (ch. 10).
