7. The Mapper algorithm

Persistence reads a cloud at all scales; Mapper (Singh, Mémoli, Carlsson, 2007) summarizes it into an interpretable graph — it is the TDA tool most used in industry, because it produces a visualization that the eye of a domain expert knows how to read: arms, flares, isolated groups.

Definition 7.1 — The Mapper pipeline Input: a cloud \(\mathcal{X}\) (or a measure), a filter function \(f : \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}^{\ell}\) (\(\ell = 1\) or 2), a cover \(\mathcal{U} = \{U_1, \dots, U_r\}\) of the image of \(f\) (overlapping intervals if \(\ell = 1\), rectangles if \(\ell = 2\)), a clustering algorithm \(\mathcal{A}\) (single-linkage, DBSCAN… cf. Machine Learning module, ch. 6). The Mapper graph is built as follows:
  1. slice: \(\mathcal{X}_i = f^{-1}(U_i)\), the fibers of the cover;
  2. cluster: \(\mathcal{X}_i = C_{i, 1} \sqcup \dots \sqcup C_{i, k_i}\) by \(\mathcal{A}\); each cluster becomes a node of the graph;
  3. connect: an edge connects \(C_{i, a}\) and \(C_{j, b}\) as soon as \(C_{i, a} \cap C_{j, b} \neq \emptyset\) — the nodes that share points.
Key parameters: the resolution (number of intervals), the gain (relative overlap, typically 10 to 50%), the filter (PCA projection, eccentricity, density, DTM…).
Why it works: Mapper ≈ dual Reeb Mapper is a discrete version of the Reeb graph of \(f\) — the quotient of \(M\) by the relation "same level connected component". The overlapping cover plays the role of continuity: the nerve theorem (2.3) adapted to fibers guarantees that, under good hypotheses (contractible intervals and intersections), the Mapper graph preserves the homotopy of the original space. With \(\ell = 1\), an annulus of data gives a cycle in the Mapper graph — the hole becomes visible to the naked eye, without computing any homology: this is persistence "for humans".
Example 7.2 — Breast cancer (Nicolau, Levine, Carlsson, 2011) Data: gene expression (24,479 dimensions) for 295 breast tumors. Filter: principal components 3 and 4 of the PCA — the first two only showed what standard analyses already saw; clustering: single-linkage. Result: the Mapper graph shows a main population and a separate arm — ~7% of the patients, labeled ER+ but with much better survival, with a distinct mutational profile ("c-MYB+" group). No classical clustering method detached this subgroup, which overlaps the main cloud in every 2D projection: Mapper separates it thanks to the topology of the cover, not thanks to distance alone. This is the paper that brought TDA into biology journals (PNAS 2011).
Mapper vs classical clustering — and the Graph ML link A k-means (Machine Learning module, ch. 6) partitions into k balls; Mapper partitions by fiber then glues back by intersection: a point can belong to several clusters, and the result is a graph whose components, bridges and cycles are exploitable (Graph ML module, ch. 0). The choice of filter is as important as the architecture of a network: eccentricity → extremes; density → modes; DTM → robustness to outliers (ch. 6).
