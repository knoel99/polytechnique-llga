9. Applications and LLGA connections

  • 3D molecules: a molecule is an equivariant cloud (EGNN, ch. 7) — conformer prediction (equilibrium geometries minimize energy; generation by equivariant diffusion meets the Multimodal GenAI module, ch. 7); properties (solvability, toxicity) via geometric descriptors (molecular HKS/WKS);
  • Proteins: binding interfaces = correspondence of molecular surfaces (shape complementarity — partial functional maps); docking; geometry meets the master's molecule-graph pipeline (Graph ML);
  • Industry: CAD/mesh retrieval, 3D scanning (reverse engineering: Poisson reconstruction of ch. 5), quality control by comparison to tolerances, dental/orthopedics (crowns = quasi-isometric shapes); autonomous vehicles (lidars = clouds → PointNet++/DGCNN); AR/VR: avatars (human body correspondence — FAUST in production), mesh compression (spectral coordinates);
  • Shape generation: diffusion on point clouds and meshes (noising/denoising positions and connectivities — exactly the DDPM of the Multimodal GenAI module, ch. 3, in \(\mathbb{R}^{3n}\) with equivariance); the "shape-GPT" future: surface tokens;
  • Connection 1 — graphs: a mesh is a graph; the operators (Laplacian, diffusion, eigendecomposition) are shared with Graph ML/Advanced GNN;
  • Connection 2 — OT: shape correspondence ≈ transport; Gromov-Wasserstein compares intrinsic metrics (OT ch. 5);
  • Connection 3 — LLM: multimodal LLMs are starting to "see" objects; 3D tokens (surface patches) join image tokens; a "shape-GPT" that generates geometry? — a cross-cutting project topic.
