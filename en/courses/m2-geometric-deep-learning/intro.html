Introduction and overview

ModuleAnalysis and Deep Learning on Geometric Data
CodeCSC_53431_EP
Team and workloadInstructor: Maks Ovsjanikov (École Polytechnique, LIX — Geometric Data Analysis group) · 24 h · 2 ECTS · discrete differential geometry, shape operators, functional maps, then deep learning on surfaces: classification, correspondence, parameterization.
Status in the programM2

This module deals with data that has a shape: 3D surfaces, meshes, and geometric data. It first builds the toolbox — discrete differential geometry, the Laplace-Beltrami operator, shape descriptors and distances, functional maps — then deep learning directly on surfaces, up to Euclidean equivariance. It is the master's gateway between geometry, spectral analysis, and learning, taught by one of the pioneers of functional maps.

What you will be able to do

  • Discretize the notions of differential geometry on meshes.
  • Compute and interpret the Laplace-Beltrami operator and the spectrum of a shape.
  • Build shape descriptors and distances that are comparable across surfaces.
  • Put shapes in correspondence through functional maps.
  • Parameterize and process surfaces to make them usable.
  • Train networks that operate directly on surfaces.
  • Understand Euclidean equivariance and its role in geometric learning.

Where this course fits in the program

M2 module (period 1), 24 h for 2 ECTS. Two M1 courses prepare you for it: Digital Representation and Analysis of Shapes for surface representation and Graph Machine and Deep Learning for Generative AI, whose spectral graph Laplacian foreshadows the Laplace-Beltrami operator; chapter 0 reactivates spectrum, surfaces, and vector calculus. Downstream, it prepares Graph Generative Models and Applications — where 3D molecules become graphs to be generated — and is one of the entry routes toward the research track of the master.

Course outline

  • Reminders: spectrum, surfaces, vector calculus — the mathematics of the module, put back in hand on surface examples.
  • Discrete differential geometry — transposing curvatures and derivatives to the discrete world of meshes.
  • The Laplace-Beltrami operator — the central spectral operator of shape analysis.
  • Shape descriptors and distances — comparing surfaces through computable signatures.
  • Functional maps — putting shapes in correspondence by transporting functions.
  • Parameterization and processing — flattening and parameterizing surfaces to process them.
  • Deep learning on surfaces — defining and training networks directly on geometry.
  • Euclidean equivariance — building models that respect the symmetries of space.
  • Research of the teaching team — the papers of the Geometric Data Analysis group, read with the tools of the module.
  • Applications and LLGA connections — where geometry meets the other tracks of the master.
  • Exercises — spectral computations and correspondences to carry out on real meshes.
  • References — the foundational papers of geometric analysis and learning on shapes.

How to study this module

  • Connect each new operator to its graph cousin: the Graph ML Laplacian is the best anchor point of the module.
  • Visualize: eigenfunctions of the Laplace-Beltrami operator, heat kernels, and descriptors are understood as colors on a mesh.
  • Implement the descriptors on small meshes before moving on to full datasets.
  • Redo chapter 0 without hesitation: vector calculus and spectra are used in every chapter.
  • Read the foundational papers on functional maps after the corresponding chapter — the course provides a guided reading of them.
  • Project yourself early onto the applications of the last chapter: that is where internship and research topics take shape.
This module in the LLGA master Geometry is the master's third structured data type, alongside graphs and text: here, the spectral analysis of shapes meets deep learning and prepares the 3D molecule generation of the Graph Generative Models module. This rare intersection — geometry, graphs, and generative models — opens original research avenues and careers where 3D data is central, from vision to design.
