0. Background: from word2vec to transformers

Prerequisites (M1 modules)
  • Embeddings (NLP ch. 3): word2vec/GloVe — a word = a static vector, derived skip-gram loss and negative sampling;
  • Attention & transformers (Deep Learning ch. 6): \(\mathrm{softmax}(QK^\top/\sqrt{d_k})V\), multi-head, residuals, LayerNorm; the "token graph" reading; BERT/GPT seen as applications (NLP ch. 7);
  • Retrieval (NLP ch. 2): TF-IDF and BM25 — the baselines of this module's retrieval (ch. 7); SBERT sentence embeddings (NLP ch. 7) for dense search;
  • RL (RL module): policies, policy gradients, PPO (ch. 4 of the RL module — the engine of alignment, ch. 5), bandits (ch. 6);
  • Cross-entropy and perplexity (ML ch. 3, NLP ch. 0): the pre-training loss is exactly maximum likelihood of the next token; \(\mathrm{PPL}=\exp(\mathcal{L})\) in base e.

The trajectory: n-grams → static embeddings → recurrent networks (LSTM) → attention (Bahdanau 2014) → transformer (Vaswani 2017) → BERT/GPT (2018-2019) → scaling (GPT-3, 2020) → alignment (InstructGPT 2022, RLHF) → general-purpose, multimodal, reasoning and agentic LLMs (2023-2026). This module covers every technical stage of this stack, from the tokenizer (ch. 1) to safety (ch. 9), proving results that M1 left as claims.

Module methodology Each chapter alternates proved theory (thm/prop boxes with proofs) and labs (nanoGPT, toy DPO, RAG, agent). The "NLP ch. N" references point to the M1 module Introduction to Text Mining and NLP, of which this course is the direct continuation: tokenization (NLP ch. 1 → ch. 1), BM25 (NLP ch. 2 → ch. 7), sentence embeddings (NLP ch. 7 → ch. 7), transformers (NLP ch. 7 → ch. 2).
