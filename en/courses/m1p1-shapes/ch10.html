10. Research of the teaching team

Mathieu Desbrun — discrete differential geometry M. Desbrun (Caltech / Inria associate) is one of the founders of discrete exterior calculus and of the discrete geometric operators that structure this module:
  • Discrete Exterior Calculus (with A. Hirani, M. Leok, J. Marsden, 2005) — arXiv math/0508341: a theory of differential forms on simplicial complexes that preserves the identities of the continuum exactly (Stokes, Hodge): the cotangent Laplacians of chapter 5 and the curvatures of chapter 7 are instances of it;
  • Discrete Differential Forms for Computational Modeling (with E. Kanso, Y. Tong) — Springer chapter: the pedagogical version (why the "naive" discretization of operators loses the structure);
  • The course Digital Geometry Processing with Discrete Exterior Calculus (K. Crane, F. de Goes, M. Desbrun, P. Schröder, SIGGRAPH Asia 2013) — ACM DL / open DDG notes: the reference for the labs (libigl), from the Laplacian to geometric flows.
Pooran Memari — topology and reconstruction of shapes CNRS senior researcher at LIX (École Polytechnique), P. Memari works at the interface of algorithmic geometry / topology:
  • Geometric Tomography with Topological Guarantees (with O. Amini, J.-D. Boissonnat, Discrete & Computational Geometry, 2013) — arXiv / Springer: reconstructing a 3D solid from 2D cross-sections (serial microscopy); the analysis shows why naive reconstructions fail (two solids of different topologies can have the same cross-sections) and gives guarantees — exactly the "sample, reconstruct, guarantee" program of chapter 4;
  • Her HDR thesis, "Points, Patterns and Shapes: towards accessible geometric modeling" (2024), synthesizes this program: point patterns (sampling, chapter 4), proximity structures (Voronoi/Delaunay, chapters 2–3), accessibility of modeling tools — page: LIX;
  • Her work on distributed Delaunay triangulations (Tile & Merge, IEEE Big Data 2019) extends chapter 3 to cloud scale: meshing billions of points by cutting space into tiles whose Delaunay triangulations stitch back together.
Common thread The two research programs meet on one principle: a discretization must preserve the structure — differential identities on one side (Desbrun: exact discrete Stokes), topological invariants on the other (Memari: homeomorphism-type guarantees). This is the ridge line of the whole module: represent faithfully before analyzing, then learn — the gateway to Geometric Deep Learning (M2).
