Exercises

  1. Guided duality. For the sites \(p_1 = (0,0)\), \(p_2 = (4,0)\), \(p_3 = (0,3)\) of Example 2.4: (a) verify that \((2, 1.5)\) is at distance 2.5 from the three sites; (b) draw the three perpendicular bisectors and the unbounded cell of \(p_2\); (c) add \(p_4 = (4,3)\) — why are the four points cocircular, and which Delaunay triangulations does one obtain (lift the degeneracy by perturbation)?
  2. Empty circle criterion. In a convex quadrilateral, \(\angle abc = 70°\) and \(\angle adc = 125°\) (diagonal \(ac\)). (a) Decide whether a flip is needed (Lemma 3.2). (b) After the flip, what is at least the smallest of the angles opposite the new diagonal? (c) Prove point (ii) of the proof: \(b \in \mathrm{disk}(a,c,d) \iff d \in \mathrm{disk}(a,b,c)\) for a convex quadrilateral.
  3. Guided lift and incircle. (a) Implement the test of Theorem 3.4: for each triangle of Delaunay (scipy), construct the plane \(\Pi\) and verify that no lifted point is under \(\Pi\) (tolerance \(10^{-12}\)). (b) Compare with the incircle determinant (signs: interior/exterior). (c) Perturb a point toward the circumcircle of a neighboring triangle: observe the flip in the computed triangulation.
  4. Lloyd. (a) Show that Lloyd relaxation decreases the quantization energy \(\mathcal{E} = \sum_i \int_{\mathcal{V}(p_i)} \|x - p_i\|^2\,dx\) (each iteration is an alternation: optimal cells for the sites, then optimal centroids for the cells). (b) Implement and plot \(\mathcal{E}\) (approximated by sampling) as a function of iterations on 100 points of the square.
  5. Guided shrinking. (a) Complete the proof of Theorem 5.2: why does mode \(j\) decay exactly like \(\rho_j^k\)? What happens for \(\lambda > 1\)? (b) Simulate a ring of radius 1, \(n = 50\), \(\lambda = 0.5\); plot the mean radius — by how much does it shrink in 100 iterations? (c) Apply Taubin \(\lambda = 0.5\), \(\mu = -0.53\) and measure.
  6. QEM. (a) Recover Example 6.3 (cube corner) in detail: write \(A, b, c\), verify \(A = I\). (b) Two perpendicular planes \(x = 0, y = 0\) plus a "ceiling" plane \(z = 1\): where does the optimal point fall and what is the cost? (c) Implement quadric edge contractions on a torus mesh (libigl) and plot the Hausdorff error as a function of the number of faces.
  7. Gauss–Bonnet. (a) Verify Theorem 7.3 on the tetrahedron and the cube (by hand), then on the minimal \(3 \times 3\) torus (9 vertices, 27 edges, 18 faces — \(\chi = 0\)). (b) Show that an edge split does not change \(\chi\) (counting). (c) Subdivide the cube 3 times and numerically verify the invariance of the total deficit.
  8. Spectra. (a) Prove the formula of Example 8.2 for the path (verify that \(u^{(k)}_i = \sin\frac{k\pi i}{n+1}\) is an eigenvector). (b) Compute the first 10 cotangent eigenvalues of a flat strip mesh and of the same bent strip (isometric); compare. (c) Discuss: why do the spectra diverge in practice (sampling, triangulation) while theory predicts equality?
  9. Guided persistence. (a) Build the alpha-shapes filtration of a noisy ring (exercise of chapter 9); extract the \(H_1\) barcode (gudhi or ripser). (b) Increase the noise until the long bar disappears: at what σ does this happen, and why is the prediction "σ ≈ hole radius / 3" reasonable? (c) Replace the ring by two rings and verify \(b_1 = 2\).
