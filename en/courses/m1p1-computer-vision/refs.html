References

  • R. Szeliski, Computer Vision: Algorithms and Applications, 2nd ed., Springer, 2022 — free online edition; the reference textbook of the course, all the classic sections (formation, filtering, geometry, stereo, motion).
  • R. Hartley, A. Zisserman, Multiple View Geometry in Computer Vision, 2nd ed., Cambridge University Press, 2004 — the bible of ch. 5-6: homographies, DLT, epipolar, autocalibration.
  • C. Harris, M. Stephens, "A Combined Corner and Edge Detector", Alvey Vision Conference, 1988 — the paper of ch. 4.
  • D. Lowe, "Distinctive Image Features from Scale-Invariant Keypoints", IJCV 60(2), 2004 — SIFT.
  • J. Canny, "A Computational Approach to Edge Detection", IEEE TPAMI 8(6), 1986 — the detector of ch. 3.
  • B. Lucas, T. Kanade, "An Iterative Image Registration Technique with an Application to Stereo Vision", IJCAI, 1981; B. Horn, B. Schunck, "Determining Optical Flow", Artificial Intelligence 17, 1981 — the two pillars of ch. 7.
  • Z. Zhang, "A Flexible New Technique for Camera Calibration", IEEE TPAMI 22(11), 2000 — the checkerboard calibration of ch. 1.
  • H. Hirschmüller, "Stereo Processing by Semiglobal Matching and Mutual Information", CVPR, 2008 — the SGM of ch. 6.
  • P. Felzenszwalb, D. Huttenlocher, "Efficient Graph-Based Image Segmentation", IJCV 59(2), 2004 — nearly linear segmentation; Y. Boykov, M.-P. Jolly, "Interactive Graph Cuts for Optimal Boundary & Region Segmentation of Objects in N-D Images", ICCV, 2001 — the interactive cuts of ch. 8.
  • O. Ronneberger, P. Fischer, T. Brox, U-Net: Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Image Segmentation, MICCAI 2015 (arXiv 1505.04597); K. He et al., Deep Residual Learning, CVPR 2016 (arXiv 1512.03385).
  • S. Ren, K. He, R. Girshick, J. Sun, Faster R-CNN, NeurIPS 2015 (arXiv 1506.01497); J. Redmon et al., You Only Look Once, CVPR 2016 (arXiv 1506.02640); N. Carion et al., DETR, ECCV 2020 (arXiv 2005.12872); A. Radford et al., CLIP, ICML 2021 (arXiv 2103.00020).
  • P. Besl, N. McKay, "A Method for Registration of 3-D Shapes", IEEE TPAMI 14(2), 1992 — ICP; Z. Zhang, "Iterative Point Matching for Registration of Free-Form Curves and Surfaces", IEEE TPAMI 13(2), 1992 — the robust variant; C. R. Qi et al., PointNet, CVPR 2017 (arXiv 1612.00593).
  • Z. Shen, J. Feydy, P. Liu, A. Curial, P. Iglesias, A. Bennet, B. Kainz, "Accurate Point Cloud Registration with Robust Optimal Transport", NeurIPS 2021 — RobOT: the ICP/optimal-transport bridge of ch. 10 (and of the M1P2 module).
  • B. Vallet, M. Brédif, A. Serna, B. Marcotegui, N. Paparoditis, TerraMobilita/iQmulus urban point cloud analysis benchmark, Computers & Graphics 49, 2015; P. Biasutti, V. Lepetit, M. Brédif, J.-F. Aujol, A. Bugeau, LU-Net, ICCV Workshops 2019; P. Biasutti, J.-F. Aujol, M. Brédif, A. Bugeau, Disocclusion of 3D LiDAR point clouds using range images, ISPRS Hannover Workshop 2017 — the instructor's publications (ch. 10-11), complete on HAL.
  • G. Bradski, "The OpenCV Library", Dr. Dobb's Journal, 2000 — opencv.org: the lab tool (Sobel, Harris, SIFT, Canny, stereo, calibration).
