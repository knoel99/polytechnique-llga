1. VAEs and latent spaces

Definition 1.1 — Conditional & hierarchical VAE Conditioning the generation: \(p_\theta(x \mid c)\) with \(c\) a class, a text (encoded), another modality. Hierarchical VAEs (HiVAE) stack latents \(p(z_1 \mid z_2)\, p(z_2 \mid z_3)\dots\); VQ-VAEs quantize the latent into a learned dictionary (discrete codes — the ancestor of image tokens); VQ-VAE-2 reached image fidelity before diffusion.
What the latent buys Compressing \(512 \times 512 \times 3\) into \(64 \times 64\) latents = \(\times 64\) less computation; all large image generative models (Stable Diffusion, DALL·E 2/3) generate in latent space — the autoencoder (light) and the generator (heavy) are decoupled. The same principle applies to graphs: latent graph, discrete codes (Graph ML module, ch. 7).
Theorem 1.2 — ELBO decomposition: reconstruction + regularization $$\mathrm{ELBO}_\phi(x) = \underbrace{\mathbb{E}_{q_\phi(z\mid x)}\big[\log p_\theta(x \mid z)\big]}_{\text{reconstruction}} \;-\; \underbrace{\mathrm{KL}\big(q_\phi(z\mid x)\,\|\,p(z)\big)}_{\text{latent regularization}}.$$ Interpretation of the two forces: the first term asks for a \(z\) that explains \(x\) (auto-encoding), the second for a \(z\) that is typical under the prior. The tension between the two structures the latent space: without the KL, \(q\) can memorize ("hole" regions not covered by the prior); without reconstruction, \(z\) encodes nothing.
The decomposition is already the form obtained in thm. 0.2; what matters is seeing where each term comes from. Expand \(\mathbb{E}_{q}\big[\log \frac{p_\theta(x\mid z)\,p(z)}{q_\phi(z\mid x)}\big]\): $$\mathbb{E}_q[\log p_\theta(x\mid z)] + \mathbb{E}_q[\log p(z)] - \mathbb{E}_q[\log q_\phi(z\mid x)] = \mathbb{E}_q[\log p_\theta(x\mid z)] - \mathrm{KL}(q_\phi \| p),$$ where we recognize the definition \(\mathrm{KL}(q\|p) = \mathbb{E}_q[\log q - \log p]\). When \(p_\theta(x\mid z)\) is a diagonal Gaussian of mean \(D_\theta(z)\) and fixed variance \(\sigma^2\), the reconstruction term equals \(-\frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\|x - D_\theta(z)\|^2 + \mathrm{cst}\): the "reconstruction MSE" of the labs is nothing but the negative of a Gaussian log-likelihood. When \(q_\phi\) and \(p\) are Gaussian, the second term closes via prop. 0.4. ∎
Posterior collapse: diagnosis and remedies The decoder (often an autoregressive transformer) becomes so powerful that it ignores \(z\): \(q_\phi(z\mid x) \to p(z)\), the KL drops toward 0 and the VAE degenerates into a non-conditional model (the latent "collapses"). Causes: a strong autoregressive decoder, a dominant KL early in training, an overly simple prior.
  • KL annealing: weight the KL by a coefficient that slowly rises from 0 to 1 — let the reconstruction settle first;
  • \(\beta\)-VAE (Higgins et al., 2017): maximizing \(\mathbb{E}[\log p(x\mid z)] - \beta\,\mathrm{KL}\) with \(\beta > 1\) forces a disentangled latent (one direction = one factor of variation); in practice, against collapse one rather uses \(\beta < 1\);
  • Free bits: penalize the KL per dimension only beyond a budget \(\lambda\): \(\max(\mathrm{KL}_j, \lambda)\) — every dimension receives a free "information allowance", which prevents it from dying.
Construction 1.3 — VQ-VAE: quantizing the latent The encoder produces \(z_e(x) \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times D}\); a codebook \(\{e_1, \dots, e_K\} \subset \mathbb{R}^D\) is learned; each position is quantized to the nearest code: $$z_q(x) = e_k, \qquad k = \arg\min_j \|z_e(x) - e_j\|_2.$$ The argmin is not differentiable. One uses the straight-through estimator (gradient copy) and the total loss: $$\mathcal{L} = \log p_\theta\big(x \mid z_q\big) + \big\|\mathrm{sg}[z_e] - e\big\|_2^2 + \beta\,\big\|z_e - \mathrm{sg}[e]\big\|_2^2,$$ where \(\mathrm{sg}\) is the stop-gradient operator.
Why this three-term loss? (i) The reconstruction must be a function of the quantized codes \(z_q\) — but \(\nabla_{z_e} z_q = 0\) (argmin): we define \(\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_e} \mathrel{:=} \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_q}\) (straight-through); the estimator is biased yet empirically effective. (ii) The term \(\|\mathrm{sg}[z_e] - e\|^2\) is quadratic in \(e\) (the codes migrate toward the produced encodings); the stop-gradient symmetrically prevents the encoder from moving. (iii) The term \(\beta\|z_e - \mathrm{sg}[e]\|^2\) does the opposite: it pulls the encoder toward the codes. The two "gear" (commitment) terms synchronize encoder and dictionary, while the first term trains the decoder; together they implement a continuous K-means inside the training loop. ∎

Hierarchy and sampling. In a HiVAE, the ELBO becomes $$\mathbb{E}_{q(z_1, z_2\mid x)}\big[\log p_\theta(x \mid z_1) + \log p(z_1 \mid z_2) + \log p(z_2) - \log q_\phi(z_1 \mid x, z_2) - \log q_\phi(z_2 \mid x)\big],$$ each level compressing the previous one (the "low" scales govern composition, the "high" ones the details — exactly the levels of a U-Net). To generate: sample \(z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I)\) (or the levels of the learned prior, as in VQ-VAE-2), then decode in a single network pass; latent traversals (interpolating \(z\) between two images) are the visual test of the continuity of the learned space.

import torch

class MiniVAE(torch.nn.Module):
    """Minimal convolutional VAE for MNIST-like data (28x28)."""
    def __init__(self, d=8):
        super().__init__()
        self.enc = torch.nn.Sequential(            # x: (B,1,28,28)
            torch.nn.Conv2d(1, 32, 4, 2, 1), torch.nn.ReLU(),   # 14x14
            torch.nn.Conv2d(32, 64, 4, 2, 1), torch.nn.ReLU(),  # 7x7
            torch.nn.Flatten(), torch.nn.Linear(64*7*7, 2*d))
        self.dec = torch.nn.Sequential(
            torch.nn.Linear(d, 64*7*7), torch.nn.Unflatten(1, (64, 7, 7)),
            torch.nn.ConvTranspose2d(64, 32, 4, 2, 1), torch.nn.ReLU(),
            torch.nn.ConvTranspose2d(32, 1, 4, 2, 1), torch.nn.Sigmoid())
    def forward(self, x):
        h = self.enc(x); mu, logvar = h.chunk(2, dim=1)
        z = mu + torch.exp(0.5*logvar) * torch.randn_like(mu)   # reparametrization
        return self.dec(z), mu, logvar

def vae_loss(x, xh, mu, logvar, beta=1.0):
    rec = torch.nn.functional.binary_cross_entropy(
        xh, x, reduction="sum") / x.size(0)                      # reconstruction
    kl  = -0.5 * torch.sum(1 + logvar - mu.pow(2) - logvar.exp()) / x.size(0)
    return rec + beta * kl                                      # ELBO (prop. 0.4)
LLGA analogies The VQ-VAE manufactures image tokens: DALL·E 1 generates these tokens autoregressively (LLMs module), latent diffusion replaces them with continuous denoising (ch. 4). On graphs, the same discrete/continuous dichotomy opposes discrete VAEs and edge diffusion (Graph ML, ch. 7); in NLP, the CLIP "latent" (ch. 5) is the shared text-image space.
