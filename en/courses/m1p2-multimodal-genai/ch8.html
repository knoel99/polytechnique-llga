8. Evaluation, costs, ethics

Panel of metrics
MetricMeasuresKnown limits
FIDdistributional distance (W2/Bures on Inception features)sampling bias (underestimated at small N — always compare at equal N), sensitive to the feature backbone, blind to text
CLIPScoreprompt-image alignment (cosine)CLIP feature set; rewards clichés that are easy to describe
LPIPSdiversity / perceptual similarity between generated pairsjudge network trained on limited human judgment
ISquality × diversity (classifier KL)sensitive to the classifier's dominant class
Human studiespairwise preferences (Elo)costly, annotator biases — the final standard
The FID inherits the geometry of \(W_2\) (OT module): the part \(\mathrm{tr}\big(\Sigma_r + \Sigma_g - 2(\Sigma_r\Sigma_g)^{1/2}\big)\) is the Bures distance between covariances. Two practical pitfalls: (i) the number of samples — FID mechanically decreases with the cohort size; (ii) the features — InceptionV3 vs CLIP sometimes reorder the rankings. Always report (backbone, N, protocol).
  • Costs: diffusion training = thousands of GPU-hours; inference = N denoiser steps + decoding; the quality/latency trade-off governs the industrial choice (fast solvers, distillation, quantization);
  • Ethics — a balanced note:
    • Deepfakes: detectors (including detectors using diffusion itself), provenance;
    • Watermarking and provenance: C2PA signed metadata (an industrial coalition: camera → editor → publication), invisible watermarks robust to re-compression — none is infallible alone, defense in depth;
    • Copyright: training on web data — ongoing legal debates (opt-out, dataset licenses, filtering); on the generation side: similarity with training works must be monitored;
    • Bias: corpus biases (representation, stereotypes) reappear amplified at generation — output audits, data documentation;
    • European AI Act: a risk-tiered approach; synthetic generators are targeted there via transparency (marking of generated content, model documentation) — in connection with the M2 ethics seminar.
Project Complete pipeline: light fine-tuning of a latent diffusion model on a small thematic set (LoRA on cross-attention), with parameterizable CFG; evaluate FID/CLIPScore before/after and analyze the effect of the guidance weight \(w\) (and of schedules \(w(t)\) — cf. ch. 9).
