0. Refresher: transformers, optimization, normalization

Reminders (Deep Learning module, chapters 2–6)
  • Backpropagation: chain rule applied to the computation graph; automatic differentiation (autograd) — DL ch. 2;
  • Optimizers: SGD with momentum, Adam/AdamW (\(m_t, v_t\), bias corrections) — DL ch. 3; learning rate scheduling;
  • Attention: \(\mathrm{softmax}(QK^\top / \sqrt{d_k}) V\) — multi-head, positional encoding, the encoder-decoder architecture — DL ch. 6; the NLP module applies it to text, the M2 LLMs module at scale;
  • Normalization: batch norm (batch statistics) vs layer norm (per example, independent of the batch); residual connections;
  • Transfer learning: pre-training + fine-tuning, classification heads — DL ch. 8.

Module format: a reading seminar. Each chapter centers on a pivotal paper that we read in full: motivation, method, experiment — and we critique the protocols (datasets, seeds, compute budgets) like a reviewer. The four instructors each intervene on their own ground (chapter 10): vision and video (Kalogeiton), graphs (Lutzeyer), diffusion models (Zhu), multimodal and video generation (Wang). Chapters 7–8 equip the final project (chapter 11): replicating a published result within a given compute budget — the acid test of modern deep learning.

Definition 0.1 — Module notation A self-attention layer with \(h\) heads over a sequence of \(n\) tokens of dimension \(d\) (heads of dimension \(d_k = d / h\)); one block = multi-head attention + MLP (expansion factor \(4\)), each preceded by LayerNorm (pre-norm) and bypassed by a residual connection. Let \(L\) denote the number of blocks, \(d_{\mathrm{ff}} = 4d\). The "compute budget" of a forward pass is measured in FLOPs (floating-point operations); approximate convention: a model with \(\theta\) parameters trained on \(D\) tokens costs \(\approx 6\,\theta D\) FLOPs.
