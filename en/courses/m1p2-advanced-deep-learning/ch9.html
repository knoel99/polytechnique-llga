9. Multimodal and generative: cross-references

This chapter consolidates the bridges (the detail lives in the corresponding modules):

  • CLIP and vision-language alignment: image-text NT-Xent (ch. 4), zero-shot, the FLIP/SigLIP family — Multimodal GenAI module, chapter 5;
  • diffusion: DDPM, score matching, LDM — Multimodal GenAI module, chapters 3–4; discrete and cross-conditional diffusion is a research area of the team (Ye Zhu, ch. 10);
  • text-to-image and multimodal evaluation (FID, CLIPScore) — Multimodal GenAI module, chapters 6–8;
  • video generation and generative cinema: temporal segmentation, consistency, camera — the work of Xi Wang (ch. 10);
  • MoE at inference, LLM quantization, LLM distillation — M2 Large Language Models module, chapters 3 and 6;
  • GNNs and graphs — Graph ML module, chapters 5–7; the work of Johannes Lutzeyer (ch. 10) is connected to it.

Cross-cutting question of the module: where does the border between "advanced vision" and "multimodal" lie? Working answer: as soon as the loss puts two modalities (or two views!) in correspondence, we are in contrast/alignment; as soon as it generates, we are in the generative; the backbones (ch. 1–6) are shared by all.
