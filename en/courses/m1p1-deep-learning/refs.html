References

Textbooks and courses

  • I. Goodfellow, Y. Bengio, A. Courville, Deep Learning, MIT Press, 2016 — free online; chapters 6-8 for this module.
  • A. Zhang, Z. Lipton, M. Li, A. Smola, Dive into Deep Learning, 2023 — d2l.ai: code-first interactive course (PyTorch), very close to the labs.
  • A. Karpathy, Neural Networks: Zero to Hero — building a GPT from scratch; and his "transformer = graph" reading (Software 3.0, Stanford, 2025-2026) which grounds the LLGA bridge.
  • S. Raschka, Machine Learning with PyTorch and Scikit-Learn, Packt, 2022 — for practice.

Founding papers (course chronology)

  • G. Cybenko, Approximation by Superpositions of a Sigmoidal Function, Mathematics of Control, Signals and Systems, 1989 — theorem 1.2.
  • X. Glorot, Y. Bengio, Understanding the Difficulty of Training Deep Feedforward Neural Networks, AISTATS 2010 — Xavier initialization.
  • A. Krizhevsky, I. Sutskever, G. Hinton, ImageNet Classification with Deep CNNs, NeurIPS 2012 (AlexNet); K. Simonyan, A. Zisserman, Very Deep Convolutional Networks (VGG), ICLR 2015.
  • D. Kingma, J. Ba, Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization, ICLR 2015 — algorithm 3.4, derived step by step.
  • K. He et al., Delving Deep into Rectifiers (He init), ICCV 2015; Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition (ResNet), CVPR 2016.
  • S. Ioffe, C. Szegedy, Batch Normalization, ICML 2015; J. Ba, J. Kiros, G. Hinton, Layer Normalization, 2016; S. Santurkar et al., How Does Batch Normalization Help Optimization?, NeurIPS 2018.
  • N. Srivastava et al., Dropout, JMLR 15, 2014; I. Loshchilov, F. Hutter, Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization (AdamW), ICLR 2019.
  • D. Hendrycks, K. Gimpel, Gaussian Error Linear Units (GELU), 2016.
  • A. Vaswani et al., Attention Is All You Need, NeurIPS 2017 — the paper to read line by line.
  • J. Su et al., RoFormer: Enhanced Transformer with Rotary Position Embedding, 2021; T. Dao et al., FlashAttention, NeurIPS 2022.
  • D. Kingma, M. Welling, Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes, ICLR 2014; I. Goodfellow et al., Generative Adversarial Nets, NeurIPS 2014.
  • E. Hu et al., LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models, ICLR 2022; Aghajanyan et al., Intrinsic Dimensionality, ICML 2021.
  • L. Smith, Cyclical Learning Rates for Training Neural Networks, WACV 2017 (LR range test); T. Chen et al., Training Deep Nets with Sublinear Memory Cost, 2016 (checkpointing).

Research of the teaching team

  • F. Rousseau, M. Vazirgiannis, Graph-of-Word and TW-IDF: New Approach to Ad Hoc IR, CIKM 2013 — the "Graph of Words" of chapter 9; F. Rousseau, E. Kiagias, M. Vazirgiannis, Main Core Retention on Graph-of-Words for Single-Document Keyword Extraction, ECIR 2015.
  • D. Skianis, F. Rousseau, M. Vazirgiannis, Inductive Text Classification via Graph Neural Networks, ACL 2020.
  • G. Michel, G. Nikolentzos, M. Vazirgiannis, Path Neural Networks: Expressive and Accurate Graph Neural Networks, ICML 2023.
  • J. Lutzeyer, C. Wu, M. Vazirgiannis, Sparsifying the Update Step in Graph Neural Networks, 2021.
  • Y. Abbahaddou, J. Lutzeyer, M. Vazirgiannis, Graph Neural Networks on Discriminative Graphs of Words, NeurIPS 2023 (extended version).
  • Z. Cui, J. Lutzeyer et al., SHAKE-GNN: Scalable Hierarchical Kirchhoff-Forest Graph Neural Network, 2025.
  • Y. Zhu et al., Boundary Guided Learning-Free Semantic Control with Diffusion Models, NeurIPS 2023; Discrete Contrastive Diffusion for Cross-Modal Music and Image Generation, ICLR 2023; Diffusion in Diffusion, ICLR 2024; The Silent Assistant: NoiseQuery as Implicit Guidance, ICCV 2025.
