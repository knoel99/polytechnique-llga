Exercises / Labs

  1. Reading notes (running thread). One sheet per session (claim / evidence / costs / reproducibility / LLGA link) on the pivotal paper; two sheets graded in depth: DeepSeek-R1 (ch. 1) and G-Retriever (ch. 7).
  2. Self-consistency (ch. 1). On a GSM8K sample: greedy vs self-consistency (k = 5, 20); plot accuracy vs k and accuracy vs cost (tokens); check that voting on the final answer extracted beats voting on the raw chain — why?
  3. Toy RLVR (ch. 1). Implement a mini-GRPO (cf. LLMs module ch. 5) with a binary format reward on a small model; observe the lengthening of the generations — a domestic "aha moment"; discuss reward hacking (does the model cheat the format?).
  4. RoPE and extension (ch. 2). Numerically verify prop. 2.2 (dot products invariant under translation); extend a small model 512 → 2048 by interpolation then by YaRN; compare perplexity and a recall task (needle) before/after.
  5. Simulated ring attention (ch. 2). Implement blockwise attention with ring passing over 4 simulated "GPUs" (processes); verify exactness vs dense attention; measure communication vs compute (overlap).
  6. Agent memory (ch. 3). Build a memory stream with the recency/importance/relevance triptych (prop. 3.1); add the reflection loop; test on 20 simulated sessions; ablation of each component of the score.
  7. MoE routing (ch. 4). Load Mixtral (open weights) and instrument the router; measure the load distribution per expert over 3 corpora (code, math, prose); reproduce the specialization figure — and its variability; implement the auxiliary loss of Switch on a toy MoE and show the balancing.
  8. SSM scan (ch. 5). Implement the SSM recurrence then the prefix scan (prop. 5.2 for the LTI case; selective version for Mamba); compare the GPU memory of the three modes (recurrence / FFT convolution / scan) on L = 8k; test the exact recall of a distant association — transformer vs SSM (the ch. 5 critique).
  9. Speculative decoding (ch. 6). Code the draft/verify scheme with residual re-sampling; verify empirically thm. 6.2 (count the tokens/iteration for measured \(\alpha\)); find the optimal \(\gamma\) for \((\alpha, c)\) measured on your machine.
  10. Graph encodings (ch. 7). Reproduce "Talk like a Graph" on 3 NLGraph tasks × 4 encodings (code from ch. 7); test the hypothesis "it is the structure, not the length" by matching lengths; a light G-Retriever: replace the PCST with a weighted shortest path and compare.
  11. Agents (ch. 8). A ReAct agent with 3 tools, one of them unreliable (bad output); measure per-step \(p\) and the \(p^k\) law (prop. 8.2); add Reflexion and measure gain vs cost; multi-agent ablation: 2 "specialized" agents vs 1 agent with the same token budget.
  12. Contamination (ch. 9). The ch. 9 n-gram detector on a small corpus; build 20 re-generated variants of a toy benchmark; measure the raw/variant gap for two models — and the judges' asymmetry (alternating order) on MT-Bench-like setups.
  13. Mini-review (ch. 10). Write a 4-page "position paper": "In 2030, will knowledge live in the weights, the context or the index?" — drawing on at least six chapters of the seminar and two modules of the master.
