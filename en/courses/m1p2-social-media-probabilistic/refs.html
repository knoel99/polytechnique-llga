References

  • M. E. J. Newman, Networks, 2nd edition, Oxford University Press, 2018 — the module's textbook: chapters on random graphs, epidemics, communities.
  • R. van der Hofstad, Random Graphs and Complex Networks, Vol. 1, Cambridge University Press, 2017 — free on arXiv: the rigorous reference for ch. 1 (thresholds, Gumbel, percolation).
  • B. Bollobás, Random Graphs, 2nd edition, Cambridge University Press, 2001 — the classic; the 1959-60 results of Erdős and Rényi put in shape.
  • P. Erdős, A. Rényi, On Random Graphs I, Publicationes Mathematicae Debrecen 6, 1959 — the founding paper (connectivity threshold).
  • D. Easley, J. Kleinberg, Networks, Crowds, and Markets, Cambridge University Press, 2010 — free online: the economics/society reading (small-world, network effects).
  • M. O. Jackson, Social and Economic Networks, Princeton University Press, 2008 — the social sciences viewpoint.
  • M. Molloy, B. Reed, A Critical Point for Random Graphs with a Given Degree Sequence, Random Structures & Algorithms 6, 1995 — the configuration model (ch. 2).
  • D. J. Watts, S. H. Strogatz, Collective Dynamics of 'Small-World' Networks, Nature 393, 1998 — the small world (ch. 2).
  • A.-L. Barabási, R. Albert, Emergence of Scaling in Random Networks, Science 286, 1999 — preferential attachment (Theorem 2.5).
  • A. Clauset, C. R. Shalizi, M. E. J. Newman, Power-law Distributions in Empirical Data, SIAM Review 51, 2009 — the estimation protocol for power laws.
  • R. Pastor-Satorras, A. Vespignani, Epidemic Spreading in Scale-Free Networks, Physical Review Letters 86, 2001 — the vanishing threshold (Theorem 3.5).
  • R. Pastor-Satorras, C. Castellano, P. Van Mieghem, A. Vespignani, Epidemic Processes in Complex Networks, Reviews of Modern Physics 87, 2015 — the big survey of epidemics on graphs.
  • A. Ganesh, L. Massoulié, D. Towsley, The Effect of Network Topology on the Spread of Epidemics, IEEE INFOCOM 2005 — the spectral threshold (Theorem 3.3; team, ch. 8.1).
  • R. Karp, C. Schindelhauer, S. Shenker, B. Vöcking, Randomized Rumor Spreading, FOCS 2000 — push-pull (Theorem 4.2).
  • D. Kempe, J. Kleinberg, É. Tardos, Maximizing the Spread of Influence through a Social Network, KDD 2003 — influence maximization (Theorem 4.4).
  • G. L. Nemhauser, L. A. Wolsey, M. L. Fisher, An Analysis of Approximations for Maximizing Submodular Set Functions—I, Mathematical Programming 14, 1978 — the \(1 - 1/e\) bound.
  • P. W. Holland, K. B. Laskey, S. Leinhardt, Stochastic Blockmodels: First Steps, Social Networks 5, 1983 — the birth of the SBM.
  • B. Karrer, M. E. J. Newman, Stochastic Blockmodels and Community Structure in Networks, Physical Review E 83, 2011 — the DC-SBM.
  • A. Decelle, F. Krzakala, C. Moore, L. Zdeborová, Inference and Phase Transitions in the Detection of Modules in Sparse Networks, Physical Review Letters 107, 2011 — the threshold conjecture (ch. 5).
  • L. Massoulié, Community Detection Thresholds and the Weak Ramanujan Property, STOC 2014 — the feasibility threshold (ch. 5.3; team, ch. 8.2).
  • C. Bordenave, M. Lelarge, L. Massoulié, Non-backtracking Spectrum of Random Graphs: Community Detection and Non-regular Ramanujan Graphs, FOCS 2015; Annals of Probability 46(1), 2018 — the non-backtracking spectrum (Theorem 5.5; team, ch. 8.3).
  • E. Abbe, Community Detection and Stochastic Block Models: Recent Developments, JMLR 18(177), 2018 — the big SBM survey.
  • M. Girvan, M. E. J. Newman, Community Structure in Social and Biological Networks, PNAS 99, 2002 — modularity, the original.
  • V. D. Blondel, J.-L. Guillaume, R. Lambiotte, E. Lefebvre, Fast Unfolding of Communities in Large Networks, Journal of Statistical Mechanics, 2008 — Louvain.
  • S. Fortunato, M. Barthélemy, Resolution Limit in Community Detection, PNAS 104, 2007 — the resolution limit.
  • J. Leskovec, K. J. Lang, A. Dasgupta, M. W. Mahoney, Community Structure in Large Networks: Natural Cluster Sizes and the Absence of Large Well-Defined Clusters, Internet Mathematics 6, 2009.
  • Y. Azar, A. Z. Broder, A. R. Karlin, E. Upfal, Balanced Allocations, SIAM Journal on Computing 29(1), 1999 — power of two choices (Theorem 7.1).
  • M. Mitzenmacher, A. Richa, R. Sitaraman, The Power of Two Random Choices: A Survey of Techniques and Results, 2001 — the synthesis.
  • L. Massoulié, J. Roberts, Bandwidth Sharing: Objectives and Algorithms, INFOCOM 1999; IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking 10(3), 2002 — proportional fairness (7.3; team, ch. 8.4).
  • S. Ioannidis, L. Massoulié, A. Chaintreau, Distributed Caching over Heterogeneous Mobile Networks, SIGMETRICS 2010; Queueing Systems 72, 2012 — decentralized caching (team, ch. 8.4).
  • G. Hall, L. Massoulié, Partial Recovery in the Graph Alignment Problem, Operations Research 71(1), 2023 — graph alignment (team, ch. 8.5).
  • M. Even, A. Koloskova, L. Massoulié, Asynchronous SGD on Graphs: A Unified Framework for Asynchronous Decentralized and Federated Optimization, AISTATS 2024 — distributed learning (team, ch. 8.5).
  • R. Cosson, L. Massoulié, Collective Tree Exploration via Potential Function Method, ITCS 2024 — collective exploration (team, ch. 8.5).
  • Tools: networkx (the TME simulations); graph-tool (Bayesian SBM); EoN (epidemics).
