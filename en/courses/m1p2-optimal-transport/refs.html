References

  • G. Peyré, M. Cuturi, Computational Optimal Transport, Foundations and Trends in Machine Learning 11(5-6), 2019 — the reference of the course: ch. 2 (theoretical foundations: Kantorovich, duality, c-transform), ch. 4 (entropic regularization and Sinkhorn), ch. 8 (statistical divergences, minimax formulations: WGAN), ch. 9 (variational problems: barycenters), ch. 10 (extensions: Gromov-Wasserstein, sliced, unbalanced).
  • C. Villani, Optimal Transport: Old and New, Grundlehren 338, Springer, 2009 — for the general proofs (Brenier, Kantorovich-Rubinstein).
  • F. Santambrogio, Optimal Transport for Applied Mathematicians, Birkhäuser, 2015 — calculus of variations, Monge-Ampère formula.
  • M. Cuturi, Sinkhorn Distances: Lightspeed Computation of Optimal Transport, NIPS 2013 — the entropic regularization (ch. 3).
  • J. Franklin, J. Lorenz, "On the scaling of algorithm for transportation problems", SIAM J. Sci. Stat. Comput., 1989 — geometric convergence of Sinkhorn (th. 3.3).
  • J. Feydy et al., Interpolating between Optimal Transport and MMD using Sinkhorn Divergences, AISTATS 2019 — the debiased divergences (ch. 3).
  • A. Genevay, L. Chizat, M. Bach, G. Peyré, R. Rigollet, Sample Complexity of Sinkhorn Divergences, AISTATS 2019 — the \(n^{-1/2}\) rate (th. 4.5).
  • N. Fournier, A. Guillin, "On the rate of convergence in Wasserstein distance of the empirical measure", Probab. Theory Relat. Fields 162, 2015 — the \(n^{-1/d}\) curse (th. 4.5).
  • M. Arjovsky, S. Chintala, L. Bottou, Wasserstein GAN, ICML 2017 (proposition 4.2); I. Gulrajani et al., Improved Training of Wasserstein GANs, NeurIPS 2017 (gradient penalty); T. Miyato et al., Spectral Normalization, ICLR 2018.
  • M. Agueh, G. Carlier, "Barycenters in the Wasserstein space", SIAM J. Math. Anal. 43, 2011; M. Cuturi, A. Doucet, Fast Computation of Wasserstein Barycenters, ICML 2014; J.-D. Benamou, G. Carlier, M. Cuturi, L. Nenna, G. Peyré, Iterative Bregman Projections for Regularized Transportation Problems, 2015 — barycenters (def. 4.3).
  • N. Courty, R. Flamary, D. Tuia, A. Rakotomamonjy, Optimal Transport for Domain Adaptation, IEEE TPAMI 39(9), 2016 (ch. 4, OTDA algorithm); N. Courty, R. Flamary, A. Habrard, A. Rakotomamonjy, Joint Distribution Optimal Transportation for Domain Adaptation, NeurIPS 2017 (JDOT).
  • M. Kusner, Y. Sun, N. Kolkin, K. Weinberger, From Word Embeddings to Document Distances, ICML 2015 — WMD (example 4.4).
  • F. Mémoli, "Gromov-Wasserstein distances and the metric approach to object matching", Found. Comput. Math. 11, 2011 — GW (def. 5.1); G. Peyré, M. Solomon, A. Gramfort, G. Ferrand, Gromov-Wasserstein Averaging of Kernel and Distance Matrices, ICML 2016 (PGW); J. Solomon et al., Convolutional Wasserstein Distances, ICML 2015 (entropic GW).
  • T. Vayer, L. Chapel, R. Flamary, R. Tavenard, N. Courty, Optimal Transport for Structured Data with Application on Graphs, ICML 2019 (FGW, def. 5.3); T. Vayer et al., Sliced Gromov-Wasserstein, NeurIPS 2019 (SGW).
  • I. Redko, T. Vayer, R. Flamary, N. Courty, CO-Optimal Transport, NeurIPS 2020 (COOT, def. 5.4).
  • D. Alvarez-Melis, T. Jaakkola, Gromov-Wasserstein Alignment of Word Embedding Spaces, EMNLP 2018 — unsupervised alignment of embeddings (example 5.5).
  • M. Feldman et al., Certifying and Removing Disparate Impact, KDD 2015 (repair); M. Hardt, E. Price, N. Srebro, Equality of Opportunity in Supervised Learning, NeurIPS 2016 (equalized odds).
  • P. Gordaliza, E. Del Barrio, F. Gamboa, J.-M. Loubes, Obtaining Fairness using Optimal Transport Theory, ICML 2019 (repair by barycenter, th. 6.2).
  • L. de Lara, A. González-Sanz, N. Asher, L. Risser, J.-M. Loubes, Transport-based Counterfactual Models, JMLR 25(136), 2024 (bicausal OT and counterfactuals, prop. 6.3).
  • J. Kleinberg, S. Mullainathan, M. Raghavan, Inherent Trade-offs in the Fair Determination of Risk Scores, ITCS 2017; A. Chouldechova, "Fair prediction with disparate impact", Big Data 5(2), 2017 — the incompatibility of the metrics (ch. 6).
  • R. Flamary et al., POT: Python Optimal Transport, JMLR 22(78), 2021 — pythonot.github.io: the tool used in all the labs (ch. 7).
  • E. Tanguy, R. Flamary, J. Delon, Reconstructing discrete measures from projections, C. R. Mathématique 362, 2024; T. Gnassounou, R. Flamary, A. Gramfort, Convolutional Monge Mapping Normalization for learning on biosignals, NeurIPS 2023 — the recent research of ch. 7.
