Exercises

  1. Bellman on paper. Analytically solve a 2-state MDP (continue at gain \(c\) per step, stop at reward 0): write the Bellman equations and compute \(V^\star\) as a function of \(\gamma\) and \(c\); interpret the indifference threshold and its link with \(\gamma\) (variant: stopping yields \(b\) once).
  2. Contraction and stopping criterion. Prove the "max-Lipschitz" lemma of Theorem 1.6, then the stopping criterion of Theorem 2.1: \(\|V_k - V^\star\|_\infty \leq \frac{\gamma}{1-\gamma}\|V_k - V_{k-1}\|_\infty\).
  3. Value vs policy iteration. On the gridworld of chapter 2, compare the number of iterations and the cost per iteration; verify that the policies coincide.
  4. Tabular Q-learning. Implement Q-learning with \(\varepsilon\)-greedy on Cliff Walking (Sutton & Barto, ex. 6.6): compare the trajectories learned by Q-learning (optimal, cliff edge) and SARSA (cautious); plot the return per episode.
  5. TD(\(\lambda\)) piecewise. Verify on an example that \(\lambda = 0\) gives back TD(0) and \(\lambda = 1\) the Monte-Carlo return; prove the telescoping \(G_t^{(n)} - V(S_t) = \sum_{l<n} \gamma^l \delta_{t+l}\) (step 1 of Proposition 3.4).
  6. Overestimation bias on an example. Let two actions with true values \(Q(s, a_1) = 0\) and \(Q(s, a_2) = 0\), estimated with independent Gaussian noises \(\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)\). Compute \(\mathbb{E}[\max(\hat Q_1, \hat Q_2)]\) (literally \(\sigma/\sqrt{\pi}\)) and comment in light of Theorem 3.5.
  7. REINFORCE, variance. On CartPole, compare REINFORCE with and without baseline; plot the variance of the empirical gradients and the learning curve.
  8. Soft policy. Derive \(\pi^\star(a|s) \propto \exp(Q(s,a)/\alpha)\) from the decomposition \(F(\pi) = -\alpha\,\mathrm{KL}(\pi\|\pi^\star) + \alpha \log Z\) (Algorithm 5.4), and verify that \(\alpha \to 0\) gives back the greedy policy.
  9. PPO-clip on paper. Plot \(\min(r\hat A, \mathrm{clip}(r, 1{-}\epsilon, 1{+}\epsilon)\hat A)\) as a function of \(r\) for \(\hat A = \pm 1\); locate the zones where the gradient is zero and relate to the proof of Theorem 4.5.
  10. Bandits. Simulate a 3-armed Bernoulli bandit (0.2, 0.5, 0.7); compare \(\varepsilon\)-greedy, UCB, Thompson; plot the cumulative regret over 5000 steps and verify the \(\log T\) behavior of the last two (compare with the Lai-Robbins bound of Theorem 6.3).
  11. Gaussian Thompson. Take up the \(\mathcal{N}(m_n, v_n)\) update of Algorithm 6.4 and verify: (a) unbiasedness of the posterior mean, (b) behavior when \(\tau^2 \to \infty\) then \(n \to \infty\).
  12. Synthetic LinUCB. Generate contexts \(x \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I_d)\), \(d = 5\), reward \(x^\top\theta^\star +\) noise; implement LinUCB and \(\varepsilon\)-greedy; plot the regret and the evolution of the bonus \(\sqrt{x^\top A^{-1} x}\).
  13. ReAct agent. Write by hand a complete ReAct trace (at least 3 Thought/Action/Observation cycles) for a multi-hop question of your choice; identify for each cycle: the observation, the action, the state estimation (POMDP belief, Def. 7.1).
  14. Agent project. Extend the guiding lab: add a verification loop (critic agent) and measure the impact on report quality (human grading or LLM-judge) and on total token cost.
Solutions — exercises 1, 2, 4 (hints), 6, 7, 10

1. States \(\{c, s\}\) (continue, stop). Bellman: \(V^\star(s) = 0\); \(V^\star(c) = \max\{0,\ c + \gamma V^\star(c)\}\). If \(c + \gamma V^\star(c) \geq 0\) then \(V^\star(c) = c/(1-\gamma)\) (geometric series), valid if and only if \(c \geq 0\); otherwise \(V^\star(c) = 0\) and the optimal policy is to stop immediately. Instructive variant: if stopping yields \(b\) once, continuing is optimal if and only if \(\frac{c}{1-\gamma} \geq b\), i.e. \(c \geq b(1-\gamma)\): the closer \(\gamma\) is to 1, the lower the threshold — weak discounting values distant gains, so one continues all the more willingly; at \(\gamma = 0\), only the immediate gain counts and the threshold is \(c \geq b\).

2. Lemma: for every \(a\), \(u_a \leq v_a + \max_b|u_b - v_b|\) so \(\max_a u_a \leq \max_a v_a + \max_b |u_b - v_b|\); symmetry. Stopping: \(\|V_k - V^\star\| \leq \|\mathcal{T}V_{k-1} - \mathcal{T}V_k\| + \|\mathcal{T}V_k - \mathcal{T}V^\star\| \leq \gamma\|V_{k-1} - V_k\| + \gamma\|V_k - V^\star\|\), hence \((1-\gamma)\|V_k - V^\star\| \leq \gamma \|V_{k-1} - V_k\|\).

4 (hints). Update \(Q(S_t, A_t)\) with the target \(R + \gamma\max_{a'} Q(S_{t+1}, a')\) (Q-learning) or \(R + \gamma Q(S_{t+1}, A_{t+1})\) where \(A_{t+1}\) is the action actually played by the \(\varepsilon\)-greedy policy (SARSA). Expected phenomenon: Q-learning converges to the optimal path along the cliff (the max "believes" the agent will never fall), SARSA to a set-back path (random exploration makes the followed policy fall); with \(\varepsilon \to 0\) the two coincide.

6. \(\max(X, Y)\) with \(X, Y\) i.i.d. \(\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)\): \(\max = \tfrac{X+Y}{2} + \tfrac{|X - Y|}{2}\); \(X + Y\) is \(\mathcal{N}(0, 2\sigma^2)\) and \(X - Y\) is \(\mathcal{N}(0, 2\sigma^2)\), so \(\mathbb{E}|X - Y| = \sqrt{2}\sigma\sqrt{2/\pi}\) and \(\mathbb{E}[\max] = \sigma/\sqrt{\pi} > 0 = \max(\mathbb{E}X, \mathbb{E}Y)\): the bias equals \(\sigma/\sqrt{\pi}\), purely created by the estimation noise — this is the continuous version of Theorem 3.5.

7. Without baseline, the estimator \(\widehat{\nabla J} = G\,\nabla\log\pi\) has variance with a factor \(\mathrm{Var}[G]\) (large: the return accumulates the noise of the whole episode); with \(b = V_w(s)\), the "value of the state" part cancels (Proposition 4.2); in practice on CartPole, learning without baseline is slow and irregular (gradient variance an order of magnitude higher), with baseline it is smooth and 3-5× faster; verify numerically that \(\widehat{\mathrm{Var}}[\hat\nabla J]\) decreases by a factor ~10 after advantage normalization.

10. Lai-Robbins for Bernoullis: the asymptotic bound is \(\sum_{a \neq a^\star} \Delta_a \mathrm{KL}(\mathrm{Bern}(\mu_a)\|\mathrm{Bern}(\mu^\star)) ^{-1} \log T\) with \(\mathrm{KL} = \mu_a \log\frac{\mu_a}{\mu^\star} + (1-\mu_a) \log\frac{1-\mu_a}{1-\mu^\star}\); compute the two KLs (arm 0.2: \(\approx 0.53\); arm 0.5: \(\approx 0.09\)), plot \(R_T / \log T\) for UCB and Thompson: both curves flatten toward the Lai-Robbins constant (Thompson closer), \(\varepsilon\)-greedy diverges linearly (regret \(\Theta(\varepsilon T)\)).
