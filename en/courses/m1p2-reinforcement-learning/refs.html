References

  • R. Sutton, A. Barto, Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction, 2nd ed., MIT Press, 2018 — free online; the textbook of the course (chapters 3-6 for ch. 1-3, chapter 13 for policy gradients).
  • M. Puterman, Markov Decision Processes: Discrete Stochastic Dynamic Programming, Wiley, 2005 — the reference for chapters 1-2 (MDP, LP, modified policy iteration).
  • D. Bertsekas, J. Tsitsiklis, Neuro-Dynamic Programming, Athena Scientific, 1996 — contraction, asynchronous DP, deadly triad.
  • C. Szepesvári, Algorithms for Reinforcement Learning, Morgan & Claypool, 2010 — compact theoretical synthesis.
  • C. Watkins, P. Dayan, "Q-learning", Machine Learning 8, 1992; J. Tsitsiklis, "Asynchronous Stochastic Approximation and Q-Learning", Mathematics of OR, 1994 — the convergences of chapter 3.
  • R. Sutton, D. McAllester, S. Singh, Y. Mansour, "Policy Gradient Methods for RL with Function Approximation", NeurIPS 1999; R. Williams, "Simple Statistical Gradient-Following Algorithms for Connectionist RL" (REINFORCE), Machine Learning, 1992 — chapter 4.
  • S. Kakade, J. Langford, "Approximately Optimal Approximate Reinforcement Learning" (CPI), ICML 2002 — the monotone improvement behind TRPO/PPO.
  • J. Schulman et al., High-Dimensional Continuous Control Using Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE), ICLR 2016; Trust Region Policy Optimization, ICML 2015; Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms, 2017 — the heart of RLHF.
  • V. Mnih et al., Human-level control through deep reinforcement learning (DQN), Nature, 2015; Asynchronous Methods for Deep Reinforcement Learning (A3C), ICML 2016.
  • H. van Hasselt, A. Guez, D. Silver, Deep Reinforcement Learning with Double Q-learning, AAAI 2016 — the max bias (Theorem 3.5) and its correction.
  • M. Hessel et al., Rainbow: Combining Improvements in Deep Reinforcement Learning, AAAI 2018 — and the original papers cited (dueling, C51, PER, noisy nets).
  • T. Lillicrap et al., Continuous Control with Deep Reinforcement Learning (DDPG), ICLR 2016; T. Haarnoja et al., Soft Actor-Critic, ICML 2018 — chapter 5.
  • S. Levine et al., Offline Reinforcement Learning: Tutorial, Review, and Perspectives, 2020; A. Kumar et al., Conservative Q-Learning for Offline Reinforcement Learning, NeurIPS 2020.
  • P. Auer, N. Cesa-Bianchi, P. Fischer, "Finite-time Analysis of the Multiarmed Bandit Problem", Machine Learning 47, 2002 (UCB1); T. Lai, H. Robbins, "Asymptotically Efficient Adaptive Allocation Rules", Advances in Applied Mathematics, 1985; O. Chapelle, L. Li, An Empirical Evaluation of Thompson Sampling, NeurIPS 2011; W. Li et al., A Contextual-Bandit Approach to Personalized News Article Recommendation (LinUCB), WWW 2010 — chapter 6.
  • S. Bubeck, N. Cesa-Bianchi, Regret Analysis of Stochastic and Nonstochastic Bandit Problems, FnT-ML, 2012 — for bandits.
  • Y. Burda et al., Exploration by Random Network Distillation, ICLR 2019 — intrinsic exploration.
  • D. Silver et al., "Mastering the Game of Go with Deep Neural Networks and Tree Search", Nature, 2016 — self-play and UCT.
  • S. Yao et al., ReAct, ICLR 2023; S. Schick et al., Toolformer, NeurIPS 2023 — and the Anthropic/LangChain multi-agent reports on graph engineering — for chapter 7.
  • Teaching team: J. Read et al., Classifier Chains for Multi-label Classification, Machine Learning, 2011; J. Read, F. Perez-Cruz, Deep Learning for Multi-label Classification, 2014; H. Gomes, J. Read et al., Machine learning for streaming data, SIGKDD Explorations, 2019; J. Read, I. Žliobaitė, Learning from Data Streams: An Overview and Update, 2022; MEKA, JMLR 2016 — chapter 8.
  • Gymnasium — the environments of the labs.
