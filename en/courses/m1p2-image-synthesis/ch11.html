11. Research of the instructor

Tamy Boubekeur — geometry for rendering, from real time to neural Professor at Télécom Paris (and Senior Principal Research Scientist at Adobe Research), T. Boubekeur builds geometric representations in the service of rendering — three milestones directly connected to the course:
  • Phong Tessellation (with M. Alexa, SIGGRAPH Asia 2008, TOG 27) — ACM DL: interpolating positions the way Phong interpolates normals — one shader line that gives coarse meshes smooth silhouettes, in real time (chapter 10): proof that "rendering" and "modeling" are one when the primitive is well chosen;
  • Sphere-Meshes (with J.-M. Thiery, E. Guy, SIGGRAPH Asia 2013, TOG 32) — ACM DL: extreme approximation of shapes by interpolation of spheres (spherical quadrics with exact error): a few dozen primitives for a character — the "proxies" of real-time rendering and scene compression (chapters 7 and 10);
  • AtlasNet (with T. Groueix, S. Chaudhuri, D. Ceylan et al., CVPR 2018) — arXiv 1802.05384: generating 3D surfaces as collections of learned parametric patches — the neural "paper-mâché": the gateway to learned 3D generation (taken up again in the Multimodal GenAI module); see also the tutorial CreativeAI: Deep Learning for Computer Graphics (Eurographics/EGSR).
The line of force From real-time tessellation to learned primitives, these works pose the same question as the module: which scene representation for which rendering budget? triangles (chapter 5), hierarchies (chapter 7), neural fields and Gaussians (chapter 9): each representational leap has moved the frontier of the possible in image synthesis — and the movement keeps accelerating.
