References

  • J. Kajiya, The Rendering Equation, SIGGRAPH 1986 — the founding paper; T. Whitted, An Improved Illumination Model for Shaded Display, CACM 1980.
  • E. Veach, Robust Monte Carlo Methods for Light Transport Simulation, Stanford PhD thesis, 1997 (MIS, MLT — the MC reference of chapter 6).
  • M. Pharr, W. Jakob, G. Humphreys, Physically Based Rendering: From Theory to Implementation (pbrt, 4th ed. online); tutorials: Ray Tracing in One Weekend; Scratchapixel.
  • B. Phong, Illumination for Computer Generated Pictures, CACM 1975; R. Cook, K. Torrance, A Reflectance Model for Computer Graphics, SIGGRAPH 1981; C. Schlick, An Inexpensive BRDF Model for Physically-based Rendering, CGF 1994; B. Burley, Physically-Based Shading at Disney, SIGGRAPH Course 2012.
  • T. Möller, B. Trumbore, Fast, Minimum Storage Ray-Triangle Intersection, JGT 1997; I. Wald, On fast Construction of SAH-based Bounding Volume Hierarchies, EGSR 2007.
  • B. Mildenhall, P. Srinivasan, M. Tancik, J. Barron, R. Ramamoorthi, N. Ng, NeRF: Representing Scenes as Neural Radiance Fields for View Synthesis, ECCV 2020; B. Kerbl, G. Kopanas, T. Leimkühler, G. Drettakis, 3D Gaussian Splatting for Real-Time Radiance Field Rendering, SIGGRAPH 2023.
  • A. Tewari et al., State of the Art on Neural Rendering, Computer Graphics Forum (EG STAR), 2020 — the survey of chapter 9 (followed by Advances in Neural Rendering, 2021).
  • C. Chaitanya, A. Kaplanyan, C. Schied, M. Salvi, A. Lefohn, D. Nowrouzezahrai, T. Aila, Interactive Reconstruction of Monte Carlo Image Sequences Using a Recurrent Denoising Autoencoder, SIGGRAPH 2017; Open Image Denoise for the labs.
  • T. Boubekeur, M. Alexa, Phong Tessellation, TOG 2008; J.-M. Thiery, E. Guy, T. Boubekeur, Sphere-Meshes: Shape Approximation using Spherical Quadric Error Metrics, TOG 2013; T. Groueix, S. Chaudhuri, D. Ceylan, et al., AtlasNet: A Papier-Mâché Approach to Learning 3D Surface Generation, CVPR 2018; tutorial CreativeAI.
