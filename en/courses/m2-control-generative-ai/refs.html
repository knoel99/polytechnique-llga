References

  • Constrained decoding: B. Willard, R. Louf, Efficient Guided Generation for LLMs, 2023 (library Outlines); Y. Dong et al., XGrammar: Flexible and Efficient Structured Generation Engine for LLMs, ICLR 2025 (code); T. Scholak, N. Schucher, D. Bahdanau, PICARD, EMNLP 2021.

  • RLHF and alignment: L. Ouyang et al., Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback (InstructGPT), NeurIPS 2022; R. Rafailov et al., Direct Preference Optimization, NeurIPS 2023; DeepSeek-AI, DeepSeekMath (GRPO), 2024 — the practical details are in the LLMs module (M2) ch. 5.
  • Watermarking: J. Kirchenbauer et al., A Watermark for Large Language Models, ICML 2023; J. Kirchenbauer et al., On the Reliability of Watermarks for LLMs, ICLR 2024; X. Zhao et al., Provable Robust Watermarking for AI-Generated Text (unigram), 2023; R. Kuditipudi et al., Robust Distortion-free Watermarks for Language Models, TMLR 2024; S. Dathathri et al., Scalable watermarking for identifying large language model outputs (SynthID-Text), Nature 634, 2024; S. Sadasivan et al., Can AI-Generated Text be Reliably Detected?, 2023; M. Christ, S. Gunn, R. Zamir, Undetectable Watermarks for Language Models, 2024 — extended by the Explainability, Security, Privacy module ch. 9.
  • Diffusion: guidance and control: J. Ho, T. Salimans, Classifier-Free Diffusion Guidance, 2022; L. Zhang, A. Rao, M. Agrawala, Adding Conditional Control to Text-to-Image Diffusion Models (ControlNet), ICCV 2023 (code); R. Mokady et al., Null-text Inversion for Editing Real Images, CVPR 2023; A. Hertz et al., Prompt-to-Prompt Image Editing with Cross-Attention Control, ICLR 2023; C. Meng et al., SDEdit: Guided Image Synthesis and Editing with Stochastic Differential Equations, ICLR 2022; T. Brooks et al., InstructPix2Pix, CVPR 2023 — foundations in the Multimodal GenAI module ch. 3-4.
  • Controlled graphs: C. Shi et al., GraphAF: a Flow-based Autoregressive Model for Molecular Graph Generation, ICLR 2020; B. Samanta et al., NeVAE: A Deep Generative Model for Molecular Graphs, AAAI 2019; C. Vignac et al., DiGress: Discrete Denoising Diffusion for Graph Generation, ICLR 2023; J. You et al., GraphRNN, ICML 2018; Y. Li et al., Deep Generative Models of Graphs (DeepGMG), ICML 2018 — developed in the Graph Generative Models module (M2).
  • Evaluation: T. Kynkäänniemi et al., Improved Precision and Recall Metric for Assessing Generative Models, NeurIPS 2019; FID and evaluation of generative models: Multimodal GenAI module ch. 8; distributional evaluation (MMD, FCD): Graph Generative Models module ch. 6.
  • Regulatory framework: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — AI Act (art. 50, transparency of generated content); OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (injections).
