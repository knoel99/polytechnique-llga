References

  • S. Shalev-Shwartz, S. Ben-David, Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms, Cambridge University Press, 2014 — free pdf (authors' website): chapters 3-6 (PAC, VC, no free lunch), 26 (Rademacher, margin); the backbone of ch. 1-6.
  • V. Vapnik, Statistical Learning Theory, Wiley, 1998 — the historical source: ERM, VC dimension, generalization bounds (ch. 1, 3, 4).
  • S. Boucheron, G. Lugosi, P. Massart, Concentration Inequalities: A Nonasymptotic Theory of Independence, Oxford University Press, 2013 — chapters 2-3 (Hoeffding, Bernstein), 5-6 (McDiarmid), 11 (maxima); the foundation of ch. 2.
  • M. J. Wainwright, High-Dimensional Statistics: A Non-Asymptotic Viewpoint, Cambridge University Press, 2019 — ch. 2 (sub-Gaussians), ch. 6 (Rademacher, margin), ch. 7 (lower bounds, Le Cam/Fano), ch. 9 (Lasso); direct extensions of ch. 5-8.
  • M. Mohri, A. Rostamizadeh, A. Talwalkar, Foundations of Machine Learning, 2nd ed., MIT Press, 2018 — Rademacher and margin bounds in textbook form (ch. 3-5, Thm 5.9 used in ch. 6).
  • L. Devroye, L. Györfi, G. Lugosi, A Probabilistic Theory of Pattern Recognition, Springer, 1996 — the classic reference on VC: Theorem 12.5 (full VC inequality, sketch of ch. 4).
  • A. B. Tsybakov, Introduction to Nonparametric Estimation, Springer, 2009 — chapter 2: Le Cam, Fano, Assouad, minimax lower bounds (ch. 7).
  • M. Ledoux, M. Talagrand, Probability in Banach Spaces: Isoperimetry and Processes, Springer, 1991 — the contraction lemma (Theorem 4.12) and the theory of empirical processes (ch. 5).
  • P. Bickel, Y. Ritov, A. Tsybakov, "Simultaneous analysis of Lasso and Dantzig selector", Annals of Statistics 37(4), 2009 — arXiv:0801.1095: the full oracle inequalities of which Theorem 8.4 is the skeleton.
  • P. Bühlmann, S. van de Geer, Statistics for High-Dimensional Data: Methods, Theory and Applications, Springer, 2011 — compatibility conditions, oracle inequalities for the Lasso (ch. 6-7).
  • T. Hastie, R. Tibshirani, M. Wainwright, Statistical Learning with Sparsity: The Lasso and Generalizations, CRC Press, 2015 — free pdf (authors' website): the "applied" companion reading of ch. 8.
  • K. Lounici, Sup-norm convergence rate and sign concentration property of Lasso and Dantzig estimators, Electronic Journal of Statistics 2, 2008 — ch. 10, paper 1.
  • K. Lounici, M. Pontil, S. van de Geer, A. B. Tsybakov, Oracle inequalities and optimal inference under group sparsity, Annals of Statistics 39(4), 2011 — ch. 10, paper 2.
  • K. Lounici, "Generalized mirror averaging and D-convex aggregation", Mathematical Methods of Statistics 16(3), 2007 — ch. 10, paper 3.
  • P. Alquier, K. Lounici, "PAC-Bayesian bounds for sparse regression estimation with exponential weights", Electronic Journal of Statistics 5, 2011 — HAL version — ch. 10, paper 4.
  • N. Cesa-Bianchi, G. Lugosi, Prediction, Learning, and Games, Cambridge University Press, 2006 — the sequential framework of exponential weights (ch. 9), sources of the "progression" inequalities.
