Open questions 2026 (i) Scaling of tabular foundations: does pre-training on synthetic priors follow exploitable scaling laws, and how far can one go in \(n\)/\(p\)? (ii) Transfer across schemas: can a model trained "cold" on millions of tables specialize in-context to a new business schema? (iii) Theory of tabular in-context: what does the PFN exactly compute — a true Bayesian average, a kernel regression, something else? (iv) Benchmarks: deduplicated suites, counted cost, industrial constraints (drift, structured missing values); cf. ch. 9. These questions feed the master's internship/lab topics.
