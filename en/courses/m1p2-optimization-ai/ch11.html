11. Research of the teaching team

The module's two instructors are among the players in optimization theory for learning. Three axes: learning under constraints (Chamon), acceleration and the fine behavior of SGD (Dieuleveut).

Luiz Chamon (CMAP, X) — learning under requirements and duality
  • L. F. O. Chamon, A. Ribeiro, Probably Approximately Correct Constrained Learning, NeurIPS 2020 — the paper that founds the generalization theory of the constrained learning of ch. 8: under representativeness conditions, every PAC-learnable class remains so under constraints, with a dual that "learns" as well as the primal.
  • L. F. O. Chamon, S. Paternain, M. Calvo-Fullana, A. Ribeiro, The empirical duality gap of constrained statistical learning, 2020 — quantifies when the empirical dual approaches the population dual: the key for multipliers learned on the training set to remain valid in test (see note of ch. 8).
  • S. Paternain, L. Chamon, M. Calvo-Fullana, A. Ribeiro, Constrained Reinforcement Learning Has Zero Duality Gap, NeurIPS 2019 — extension of the "zero gap" to constrained control: the bridge toward the Reinforcement Learning module (ch. 3–4 of this module).
Proposed cross-cutting reading: apply ch. 8 to a fairness problem (Emerging ML module, ch. 6) while measuring the empirical duality gap of exercise 8.
Aymeric Dieuleveut (CMAP, X) — optimal speed and constant-step SGD
  • A. Dieuleveut, N. Flammarion, F. Bach, Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger Convergence Rates for Least-Squares Regression, ICML 2017 — averaged SGD (Polyak–Ruppert) achieves the optimal minimax rates in regression, without knowing the regularity level: the statistical version of the "optimal rate" of ch. 3.
  • A. Dieuleveut, A. Durmus, F. Bach, Bridging the Gap between Constant Step Size Stochastic Gradient Descent and Markov Chains, Annals of Statistics 2020 — constant-step SGD (th. 5.2) converges to a stationary law; the paper gives its exact geometry and explains why the residual noise can be beneficial (prop. 5.3) — the analysis of the "constant learning rate + final decay" practice for networks.
Proposed cross-cutting reading: connect ch. 5 to the Emerging ML module (ch. 0, SGD reminders) and to the Machine Learning module (ch. 9, massive data).
