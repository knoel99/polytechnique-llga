References

Textbooks

  • T. Hastie, R. Tibshirani, J. Friedman, The Elements of Statistical Learning, 2nd ed., Springer, 2009 — free PDF: the reference textbook of the course.
  • G. James, D. Witten, T. Hastie, R. Tibshirani, An Introduction to Statistical Learning, 2nd ed., Springer, 2021 — the applied (Python) version of the same foundation.
  • C. Bishop, Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning, Springer, 2006 — for the generative viewpoint (EM, kernels) taken up again in Deep Learning.
  • S. Shalev-Shwartz, S. Ben-David, Understanding Machine Learning, Cambridge, 2014 — ch. 6 (VC dimension and Sauer's lemma), ch. 26 (Rademacher complexity), ch. 13 (margin bounds, SVM).
  • K. Murphy, Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction, MIT Press, 2022 — a modern panorama, free online.
  • S. Boucheron, G. Lugosi, P. Massart, Concentration Inequalities: A Nonasymptotic Theory of Independence, Oxford University Press, 2013 — ch. 1-2: Markov, Hoeffding, Chernoff, and beyond (chapter 0).
  • scikit-learn User Guide — the labs follow its conventions (pipelines, calibration, imblearn for imbalance).

Foundational papers cited in the course

  • J. H. Friedman, Greedy Function Approximation: A Gradient Boosting Machine, Annals of Statistics 29(5), 2001 — Project Euclid: boosting as functional gradient descent (chapter 5).
  • D. Arthur, S. Vassilvitskii, k-means++: The Advantages of Careful Seeding, SODA 2007 — PDF (Stanford): the \(8(\ln k + 2)\) guarantee (chapter 6).
  • M. E. Tipping, C. M. Bishop, Probabilistic Principal Component Analysis, JRSS-B 61(3), 1999 — Oxford Academic: PCA as a generative model (chapter 6).

Research of the teaching team

  • J. Read, B. Pfahringer, G. Holmes, R. Kirkby, Classifier Chains for Multi-label Classification, ECML PKDD 2009 — PDF: the classifier chains paper (chapter 8).
  • J. Read, Scalable Multi-label Classification, PhD thesis, University of Waikato, 2010 — full text: the transformation/adaptation taxonomy of multi-label.
  • J. Read, A. Bifet, B. Pfahringer, G. Holmes, Scalable and Efficient Multi-label Classification for Evolving Data Streams, Machine Learning 88, 2012 — multi-label on streams (iSOUP-Tree).
  • J. Read, Concept-drifting Data Streams are Time Series, 2018 — arXiv:1810.02266.
  • J. Read et al., Deep Learning for Multi-label Classification, 2014 — arXiv:1502.05988: a multi-label and deep learning panorama.
  • J. Montiel, M. Halford, D. Salles, S. Vrysis, et al. (incl. J. Read), River: machine learning for streaming data in Python, JMLR 22(110), 2021 — JMLR: the tooling of the "streaming" labs.
  • L. Montuelle, E. Le Pennec, PAC-Bayesian Aggregation of Affine Estimators, 2014 — arXiv:1410.0661; long version: PAC-Bayesian aggregation of linear estimators, Nonparametric Statistics 33, 2019.
  • J. Salmon, E. Le Pennec, An Aggregator Point of View on NL-Means, SPIE Wavelets and Sparsity XIV, 2009 — patch-based denoising reread as aggregation of estimators.
  • F. Autin, E. Le Pennec, K. Tribouley, Thresholding Methods to Estimate Copula Density, Journal of Multivariate Analysis 101(1), 2010 — thresholding and automatic selection in nonparametrics.
  • É. Gautier, E. Le Pennec, Adaptive Estimation in the Nonparametric Random Coefficients Binary Choice Model by Needlet Thresholding — HAL (inria-00601274): needlets and scale selection, link with logistic regression.
