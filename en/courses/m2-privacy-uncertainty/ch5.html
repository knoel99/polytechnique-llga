5. DP in industry and attacks

Example 5.1 — Three real deployments (a) 2020 US Census (Abowd, KDD 2018). All published statistical tables go through the TopDown algorithm (optimization + Gaussian noise, exponential mechanism for the invariants); direct motivation: reconstruction attacks on the 2010 census tabulations — an internal attack exactly reconstructed a substantial fraction of the individual records (on the order of half); per-domain public ε budget, intense public debates with demographers. (b) Apple (2017–). "Learning with Privacy at Scale": keyboard/emoji/QuickType telemetry in local DP (count-mean sketch + Hadamard); millions of devices, daily ε on the order of 4–8 per domain — an illustration of the "budget" that is spent every day. (c) Google RAPPOR (Erlingsson et al., CCS 2014). First large academic-industry production of local DP; chain: signal (Bloom register) → permanent randomized response → instantaneous RR → cohorts; real discoveries (dominant Chrome settings).
Landmark 5.2 — Two families of attacks that motivate the guarantees Membership inference (Shokri et al., IEEE S&P 2017). The adversary queries the model (confidences) and classifies "was in the train?"; AUC typically well above 0.5 on rare/atypical points — the risk grows with the model's overconfidence: calibration (ch. 7) is also a defense. Unintended memorization (Carlini et al., 2019 — "The Secret Sharer"). Insert a canary ("secret key") into the train of a generator; exposure measured by the relative perplexity of the canary: LLMs memorize and exfiltrate verbatim sequences seen only once — a founding result for LLM privacy (ch. 11); follow-up: training extraction on GPT-2 by the same group (2021). DP is the defense with a guarantee: an \(\varepsilon\)-DP mechanism bounds the membership likelihood ratio at \(e^{\varepsilon}\) (hence the AUC of any adversary); the private trained model remains useful (ch. 4) provided one pays in accuracy.
Regulation GDPR (anonymization "reasonably impossible to re-identify"): DP is the most accepted formal reading of this criterion; sectoral frameworks (health, banking) and emerging standards (NIST, ISO/IEC 27559). For the engineer: keep a budget ledger (which query, which ε spent, which composition) as one keeps an incident log — the frameworks (OpenDP, Google DP library) enforce this discipline.
