San Roman et al., Proactive Detection of Voice Cloning with Localized Watermarking (AudioSeal), ICASSP 2024; Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — AI Act (art. 50) — extended by the Explainability module ch. 9 and the Control in Generative AI module ch. 6 and 10.
