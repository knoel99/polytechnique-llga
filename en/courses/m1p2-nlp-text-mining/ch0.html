0. Review: vector spaces and probabilities

L3 background used
  • Vector spaces: basis, dimension, projection; cosine similarity \(\cos(u, v) = \frac{u^\top v}{\|u\|\,\|v\|}\) — invariant under dilation (\(\cos(u,v)=\cos(\lambda u, v)\) for \(\lambda > 0\), which "cancels out" document lengths); the \(\ell_2\) norm will be used to normalize TF-IDF vectors;
  • Linear algebra: singular value decomposition \(\mathbf{X} \approx \mathbf{U}_k \Sigma_k \mathbf{V}_k^\top\) — the engine of LSA (ch. 4) and the direct cousin of PCA (Machine Learning module);
  • Conditional probabilities and Bayes: \(\mathbb{P}(c \mid d) \propto \mathbb{P}(d \mid c)\,\mathbb{P}(c)\) — the Naive Bayes classifier (ch. 5) follows directly from it; the Dirichlet distribution \(\mathrm{Dir}(\alpha)\), conjugate of the multinomial — the foundation of LDA (ch. 4);
  • Markov chains and dynamic programming (Refresher CS): forward/backward and Viterbi (ch. 6) are Bellman recursions on a chain; the TextRank (ch. 8) is a Markov chain raised to a power;
  • Likelihood, MLE and EM: word2vec (ch. 3), LDA (ch. 4), Naive Bayes (ch. 5) and the Unigram tokenizer (ch. 1) are all likelihood maximizations — by SGD, Gibbs, direct counting or EM;
  • Entropy and perplexity: for a language model, \(\mathrm{PPL} = \exp\!\big(-\tfrac{1}{N}\sum_{t} \log p(x_t \mid x_{<t})\big)\) — the universal quality measure of a generative model of text (M2 LLMs, ch. 3).
How to read this course Each chapter follows the same triptych: (i) a representation of text (counts, weights, vectors, distributions, latent topics), (ii) a learning algorithm with its statistical justification, (iii) a quantified evaluation. The thm/prop boxes contain the proven results; the proofs come at the end of each box. References of the form "module X, chapter N" point to the other courses of the master's program; the natural extension of this module is the M2 "Large Language Models" course, which picks up tokenization (ch. 1), BM25 (ch. 2), sentence embeddings (ch. 7) and transformers where we leave them.
