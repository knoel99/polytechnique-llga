References

  • Textbooks: D. Jurafsky, J. Martin, Speech and Language Processing, 3rd ed. (draft) — free online (the module's textbook); C. Manning, H. Schütze, Foundations of Statistical Natural Language Processing, MIT Press, 1999 (HMM, CRF, collocations).
  • Tokenization: R. Sennrich et al., Neural Machine Translation of Rare Words with Subword Units (BPE), ACL 2016; T. Kudo, J. Richardson, SentencePiece, EMNLP 2018; T. Kudo, Subword Regularization (Unigram), ACL 2018.
  • Weighting and retrieval: S. Robertson, H. Zaragoza, The Probabilistic Relevance Framework: BM25 and Beyond, Foundations and Trends in IR, 2009 — doi; A. Broder, Min-wise Permutations (MinHash), 1998.
  • Embeddings: T. Mikolov et al., Efficient Estimation of Word Representations in Vector Space, 2013; O. Levy, Y. Goldberg, word2vec Explained, 2014; J. Pennington et al., GloVe, EMNLP 2014; P. Bojanowski et al., Enriching Word Vectors with Subword Information (fastText), TACL 2017; S. Arora, Y. Liang, T. Ma, A Simple but Tough-to-Beat Baseline for Sentence Embeddings, TACL 2017; A. Caliskan et al., Semantics derived automatically from language corpora contain human-like biases, Science 2017; T. Bolukbasi et al., Man is to Computer Programmer as Woman is to Homemaker, NeurIPS 2016.
  • Topic models: D. Blei, A. Ng, M. Jordan, Latent Dirichlet Allocation, JMLR 2003; T. Griffiths, M. Steyvers, Finding scientific topics, PNAS 2004 (Gibbs); J. Chang et al., Reading Tea Leaves: How Humans Interpret Topic Models, NeurIPS 2009.
  • Classification, sequences, transformers: A. Joulin et al., Bag of Tricks for Efficient Text Classification, EACL 2017; Y. Kim, Convolutional Neural Networks for Sentence Classification, EMNLP 2014; J. Lafferty et al., Conditional Random Fields, ICML 2001; J. Devlin et al., BERT, NAACL 2019; Y. Liu et al., RoBERTa, 2019; C. Raffel et al., T5, JMLR 2020; N. Reimers, I. Gurevych, Sentence-BERT, EMNLP 2019; L. Martin et al., CamemBERT, ACL 2020; H. Le et al., FlauBERT, ACL 2020.
  • Industrial text mining: R. Mihalcea, P. Tarau, TextRank, 2004; S. Rose et al., Automatic Keyword Extraction from Individual Documents (RAKE), 2010; R. Campos et al., YAKE!, Information Processing & Management 2020 (code).
  • Research of the teaching team (ch. 9): Rousseau & Vazirgiannis (TW-IDF, CIKM 2013); Tixier, Malliaros & Vazirgiannis (degeneracy, EMNLP 2016); Skianis et al. (EMNLP 2016); Giatsidis et al. (CoreCluster, AAAI 2014); Abdine, Buscaldi et Vazirgiannis (WSI, AI Open 2023); Douka et al. (JuriBERT, 2021); Buscaldi et al. (JIIS 2011; WWW 2015; TexTrolls; generated-text detection; memorization; MUCH).
  • Tools: HuggingFace Course (the labs follow this curriculum); spaCy; gensim; rank_bm25; datasketch (MinHash LSH).
