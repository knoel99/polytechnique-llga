Questions a LLGA graduate should be able to answer — industry framing

This page is not a course quiz. It lists field questions (deployment, scale, safety, cost, governance) that show up in companies and applied labs. Each entry quietly points to the modules that prepare you; links lead to existing notes.

LLM deployment & generation systems

How do you choose between a proprietary API, a hosted open-weights model, and local fine-tuning for a given business case?

Unit cost, latency, prompt confidentiality, vendor lock-in, and the ability to detect drift: the decision is not only “model quality”.

Modules: Large Language Models · ML Ops and LLM Engineering · Advanced Topics in LLMs

How do you design a RAG (or graph-augmented) pipeline that stays reliable when the corpus changes every week?

Indexing, chunking, hallucination evaluation, answer monitoring, and recovery after a documentation incident.

Modules: Large Language Models · Text Mining & NLP · Graph ML for Generative AI · Graph Generative Models

What guardrails belong around a production LLM to limit data leaks and out-of-scope use?

Input/output filtering, logging, system-prompt policies, adversarial tests, and acceptance criteria before go-live.

Modules: Explainability, Security, Privacy of LLMs · Security and Robustness for AI · Privacy and Uncertainty

Graphs at scale

When does a GNN (or a graph + LLM pipeline) deliver a real gain over a tabular or text-only model?

Homophily, density, edge noise, ingestion cost, and the need for explainability: knowing when to refuse a graph “because it is trendy”.

Modules: Graph ML for Generative AI · Advanced Graph Neural Networks · Geometric Deep Learning

How do you move from a mid-size graph prototype to an industrial graph (billions of edges, continuous updates)?

Partitioning, neighborhood sampling, stale features, inference latency, and eventual consistency of embeddings.

Modules: Advanced GNN · Graph Generative Models · DataBase Management System

Safety, robustness, privacy

How do you measure and improve robustness under adversarial attacks, distribution shift, and out-of-domain inputs?

Benchmarks, red teaming, calibrated uncertainty, and abstention criteria rather than a confident but wrong answer.

Modules: Security and Robustness · Privacy and Uncertainty · Statistical Learning Theory

Which privacy guarantees (anonymization, differential privacy, federated access) are realistic for a sensitive business dataset?

Utility/privacy trade-off, ε budgets, leakage via LLM memorization, and legal duties turned into technical constraints.

Modules: Privacy and Uncertainty · Explainability, Security, Privacy of LLMs · EA Collaborative Learning

MLOps, cost, energy

How do you budget and reduce inference cost (tokens, GPU-hours, cache, distillation) without breaking the business SLA?

Profiling, batching, quantization, cascaded smaller models, and per-request cost tracking in production.

Modules: ML Ops and LLM Engineering · Optimisation and Responsible AI · Optimization for AI

What does a “reproducible and auditable” ML/LLM pipeline require (data, features, prompts, models, evaluations)?

Versioning, non-regression tests, feature stores / prompt registries, and evidence for an internal or regulatory audit.

Modules: ML Ops and LLM Engineering · Machine Learning · Statistics in Action

How do you estimate the energy / carbon footprint of training or an inference service and use it in architecture choices?

Measurement (not only order-of-magnitude), accuracy/cost trade-offs, and reporting that business stakeholders can read.

Modules: Optimisation and Responsible AI · ML Ops and LLM Engineering

Multimodal & agents

When do you need a multimodal model (vision–language–audio) rather than an orchestrated chain of specialists?

Modality alignment, latency, annotation cost, and the risk of a single black box versus composable pipelines.

Modules: Multimodal Generative AI · Computer Vision Applications · Speech Technology

How do you scope an autonomous agent (tools, memory, policies) so it is useful without becoming an operational risk?

Action perimeter, human validation, trace observability, and stop criteria when the agent drifts.

Modules: RL and Autonomous Agents · Control in Generative AI · Advanced Topics in LLMs

Governance & accountability

How do you explain a model decision (or an LLM generation) to a business owner, a lawyer, or a regulator without overselling interpretability?

Explanation levels adapted to the audience, limits of post-hoc methods, and documented assumptions.

Modules: Explainability, Security, Privacy of LLMs · Machine Learning · Optimal Transport & Fairness

Which indicators and processes should govern a model portfolio (lifecycle, risk, compliance)?

Inventory, risk classification, periodic reviews, and alignment with the organization’s data / AI strategy.

Modules: ML Ops and LLM Engineering · Security and Robustness · Optimisation and Responsible AI

These questions do not replace exams or the official courses of École Polytechnique. They are a compass to connect the LLGA curriculum to issues that show up in production.
