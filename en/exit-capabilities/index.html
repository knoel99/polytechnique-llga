LLM deployment & generation systems

Proprietary API, hosted open-weights, or local fine-tuning — how do you decide for a business case?

After this track, one should be able to compare unit cost, latency, prompt confidentiality, vendor lock-in and drift detection — beyond a public leaderboard score.

Typical scenario: legal blocks any document leaving the VPC; the business wants a demo in two weeks. What is the minimal viable path, and what are later switch criteria?

Modules (notes): Large Language Models · ML Ops and LLM Engineering · Advanced Topics in LLMs

How do you keep a RAG (or graph-augmented) pipeline reliable when the corpus changes every week?

Indexing, chunking, hallucination evaluation, answer monitoring, recovery after a documentation incident — topics the text / graph / LLM modules prepare, and industry hits continuously.

Scenario: Confluence doubled; answers cite archived pages. What do you measure first, and what do you automate?

Modules: Large Language Models · Text Mining & NLP · Graph ML for Generative AI · Graph Generative Models

What guardrails belong around a production LLM to limit leaks and out-of-scope use?

Input/output filtering, logging, system-prompt policies, adversarial tests, acceptance criteria before go-live — a natural extrapolation of safety / privacy modules.

Modules: Explainability, Security, Privacy of LLMs · Security and Robustness for AI · Privacy and Uncertainty

How do you evaluate an LLM on a business task without trusting the public leaderboard?

Proprietary test sets, calibrated human / LLM-as-judge protocols, regression metrics, and tracking over time — a cross-cutting LLM + stats + MLOps skill.

Scenario: stakeholders say “it feels better” after a model swap. How do you decide with a reproducible protocol in one week?

Modules: Large Language Models · Statistics in Action · ML Ops and LLM Engineering

Graphs at scale

When does a GNN (or graph + LLM) beat a tabular or text-only model?

Homophily, density, edge noise, ingestion cost, need for explainability — including knowing when to refuse a graph “because it is trendy”.

Modules: Graph ML for Generative AI · Advanced Graph Neural Networks · Geometric Deep Learning

How do you move from a mid-size prototype to an industrial graph (billions of edges, continuous updates)?

Partitioning, neighborhood sampling, stale features, inference latency, embedding consistency — extrapolation beyond toy lab graphs.

Modules: Advanced GNN · Graph Generative Models · DataBase Management System

How do you connect a knowledge graph and an LLM without duplicating business truth in the prompt?

Entity linking, structured queries vs free generation, ontology governance, and graph maintenance cost — a frequent “enterprise RAG” question.

Modules: Graph ML · Large Language Models · Graph Generative Models

Safety, robustness, privacy

How do you measure and improve robustness under attacks, distribution shift, and out-of-domain inputs?

Benchmarks, red teaming, calibrated uncertainty, abstention criteria rather than a confident wrong answer.

Modules: Security and Robustness · Privacy and Uncertainty · Statistical Learning Theory

Which privacy guarantees are realistic for a sensitive business dataset?

Anonymization, differential privacy, federated access, leakage via LLM memorization, and turning legal constraints into technical choices — without promising “absolute privacy”.

Modules: Privacy and Uncertainty · Explainability, Security, Privacy of LLMs · EA Collaborative Learning

How do you respond to an incident where the model emitted PII or unsafe advice?

Extrapolate: incident playbook, rollback, root-cause analysis, business communication, and fixes (filters, fine-tuning, refusals). Natural link between safety and MLOps.

Modules: Security and Robustness · ML Ops and LLM Engineering · Explainability, Security, Privacy of LLMs

MLOps, cost, energy

How do you budget and cut inference cost without breaking the business SLA?

Tokens, GPU-hours, cache, distillation, quantization, cascaded models — engineering skills that MLOps / optimization / responsible-AI modules make approachable.

Modules: ML Ops and LLM Engineering · Optimisation and Responsible AI · Optimization for AI

What does a reproducible, auditable ML/LLM pipeline require?

Versioning of data / features / prompts / models / evaluations, non-regression tests, evidence for an internal audit — industrial extrapolation of “good pipelines” from coursework.

Modules: ML Ops and LLM Engineering · Machine Learning · Statistics in Action

How do you estimate energy / carbon footprint and use it in architecture trade-offs?

Measurement (not only order-of-magnitude), accuracy/cost trade-offs, reporting business stakeholders can read.

Modules: Optimisation and Responsible AI · ML Ops and LLM Engineering

Multimodal & agents

Single multimodal model or an orchestrated chain of specialists?

Modality alignment, latency, annotation cost, single black-box risk vs composable pipelines — an architecture question, not only a SOTA-paper question.

Modules: Multimodal Generative AI · Computer Vision Applications · Speech Technology

How do you scope an agent (tools, memory, policies) so it stays useful without becoming an operational risk?

Action perimeter, human validation, trace observability, stop criteria when the agent drifts — extrapolation from RL / control / advanced LLM modules.

Scenario: the agent can open tickets and send email. Where do guardrails go on day one?

Modules: RL and Autonomous Agents · Control in Generative AI · Advanced Topics in LLMs

Governance & accountability

How do you explain a model decision (or a generation) to business, legal, or a regulator without overselling interpretability?

Explanation levels adapted to the audience, limits of post-hoc methods, documented assumptions.

Modules: Explainability, Security, Privacy of LLMs · Machine Learning · Optimal Transport & Fairness

Which indicators and processes should govern a model portfolio?

Inventory, risk classification, periodic reviews, alignment with data / AI strategy — something one should be able to sketch after this track, without speaking for a legal department.

Modules: ML Ops and LLM Engineering · Security and Robustness · Optimisation and Responsible AI

How do you prioritize an AI roadmap when everyone asks for an “internal GPT”?

Extrapolate: business value, available data, risk, inference cost, technical debt. A Graph+LLM+MLOps synthesis skill — not an official degree checklist.

Modules: Large Language Models · ML Ops · Machine Learning · Graph ML
