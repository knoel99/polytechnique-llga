Graph × LLM on a business case

When does a graph + LLM pipeline beat tabular or text-only models on a business case (fraud, recommendation, knowledge graph)?

The LLGA core is exactly this diagnosis: homophily, relational structure, textual vs structural signal. After the track, one should argue why adding a GNN or a document graph changes (or does not change) the business decision.

Payment fraud: account features alone vs co-occurrence graph + LLM on transaction text. Where does message-passing yield a measurable lift?

Robustness, privacy, explainability (graph / generation context)

How do you assess robustness or privacy of an LLM inside a graph-augmented pipeline or controlled generation (M2 modules in this repo)?

Not generic “standalone LLM safety”: attacks on graph retrieval, leakage via sensitive subgraphs, explainability of message-passing paths + LLM citations, uncertainty on OOD nodes.

The model cites a confidential KG neighbor. Is that an LLM filter issue, a graph access-policy issue, or both?

How do you explain a decision that combines a GNN score and an LLM generation to business stakeholders — without overselling interpretability?

Typical LLGA synthesis: edge/subgraph attributions, retrieval traces, limits of post-hoc LLM explanations. Explainability + Graph ML + ML (statistical frame) prepare that discourse.

Graph×LLM research problematics (illustrative selection)

Illustrative selection of recent theses / manuscripts linking the LLGA track to active research questions. This is not an exhaustive catalog and not an official degree opinion — extrapolated study notes from verified open sources.

How do you integrate HGNNs / GNNs and LLMs into a knowledge-graph engine at scale (Graph-RAG / KGQA)?

Typical LLGA issue: train heterogeneous GNNs on a KG engine without exporting the full graph, task-oriented sampling, and KGQA when the graph is incomplete.

Verified reference: Hussein Abdallah, AI-Enabled KG Platform: Incorporating LLMs and GNNs into KG Engines (Ph.D., Concordia University, 2025) — Spectrum Concordia.

Modules: Graph ML · Advanced GNN · Large Language Models · ML Ops and LLM Engineering

How do you improve LLM reasoning, transparency and robustness with neuro-symbolic meaning representation frameworks (MRFs)?

MRFs encode semantics as graphs; coupling them with LLMs (parsing, NLI, formal solving) is an explainability path beyond opaque prompting — NLP × structure at the core of the track.

Verified reference: Zacchary Sadeddine, Meaning Representation Frameworks and Reasoning in the Era of Large Language Models (IP Paris / Télécom Paris, 2025) — theses.fr/2025IPPAT028.

Modules: Large Language Models · Text Mining & NLP · Explainability, Security, Privacy of LLMs

How do you semantically measure and enrich embeddings for link prediction in knowledge graphs?

Beyond classic rank metrics: semantic metrics (e.g. Sem@K), schema-rich resources, neuro-symbolic methods — reads directly on Graph ML / Advanced GNN / learning theory.

Verified reference: Nicolas Hubert, Mesure et enrichissement sémantiques des modèles à base d’embeddings pour la prédiction de liens dans les graphes de connaissances (Université de Lorraine, 2024) — theses.fr/2024LORR0059.

Modules: Graph ML · Advanced GNN · Statistical Learning Theory

How do you automate interpretable feature engineering by combining knowledge graphs, RL and LLMs?

KG-guided feature engineering, RL policies, and LLM generation with symbolic guardrails — where RL + Graph ML + explainability in the curriculum meet.

Verified reference: Mohamed Bouadi, Exploitation des graphes de connaissance pour automatiser l’ingénierie des fonctionnalités et améliorer l’interprétabilité… (Université Paris Cité, 2024) — theses.fr/2024UNIP7218.

Modules: RL and Autonomous Agents · Graph ML · Explainability, Security, Privacy of LLMs · Large Language Models

How do you make a KG reliable (errors, inductive completion) then inject it into an LLM (structured prompting such as KnowGPT)?

Error detection → completion → KG prompting to reduce hallucinations: a central Graph×LLM problem, not generic text RAG.

Verified reference (arXiv manuscript/preprint; do not invent affiliations absent from the notice): Qinggang Zhang et al., KnowGPT line — Enhancing Large Language Models with Reliable Knowledge Graphs — arXiv:2506.13178.

Modules: Large Language Models · Graph ML · Privacy and Uncertainty

How do you fuse language models and knowledge graphs for more reliable AI reasoning?

Systems thesis on LLM↔KG reasoning — a direct research mirror of the LLGA “graphs and applications” core.

Verified reference (PDF): Konstantinos (Costas) Mavromatis, Fusion of Knowledge: Enhancing AI Reasoning through Language Models and Knowledge Graphs (Ph.D., University of Minnesota, June 2024) — UMN Conservancy PDF.

Modules: Graph ML · Large Language Models · Advanced Topics in LLMs

Which deep architectures and latent geometries advance geometric deep learning beyond “flat” GNNs?

Geometric inductive biases, deep architectures, latent geometry — ties to Geometric DL / Advanced GNN / shapes–TDA in the track.

Verified reference: Jiaxu Liu, Advancing Geometric Deep Learning: Deep Architectures and Latent Geometry (Ph.D., University of Liverpool, 2025) — Liverpool Repository.

Modules: Geometric Deep Learning · Advanced GNN · Shapes · TDA

How do you combine LLMs and GNNs to fuse multimodal representations over semi-structured data (documents, tables, images)?

Mixed text/image/structure documents: GNN carries spatial structure, LLM carries meaning — multimodal × graph extrapolation. Status: PhD project (registered), not yet a defended thesis.

Verified reference: Raphaël Hatté, Combinaison LLM et GNN pour la fusion de représentations multimodales… (PhD project, Chambéry / USMB) — theses.fr/s428624.
