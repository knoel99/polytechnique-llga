Skill checkpoints

Five questions to test whether the track delivered its core skill: arguing, with evidence, where graphs and LLMs change the answer — and where they do not. Inferred from the curriculum and these notes; not an official exam.

  1. When does a graph + LLM pipeline beat tabular or text-only models on a business case (fraud, recommendation, knowledge graph)? — argue the lift, or its absence. Notes: Graph ML · LLMs · Advanced GNN
  2. How do you design graph-augmented RAG (retrieval over a document/entity graph) rather than flat vector RAG — and when is the flat version enough? Notes: Graph ML · NLP · LLMs
  3. How do you size a GNN stack for an industrial graph (neighborhood sampling, over-squashing, depth) instead of “just add layers”? Notes: Advanced GNN · DBMS
  4. How do you assess robustness and privacy of an LLM inside a graph-augmented pipeline — attacks on graph retrieval, leakage via sensitive subgraphs? Notes: LLM XSP · Security · Privacy
  5. How do you explain a decision that combines a GNN score and an LLM generation to business stakeholders — without overselling interpretability? Notes: LLM XSP · ML
