Why one notation

The same object comes back in many courses. A student who meets PageRank in Graph ML and then APPNP in Advanced GNN should not have to check whether \alpha is the chance of following an edge or of jumping away. When one symbol carries two meanings, the reader pays twice: once to translate, once to doubt the result.

These notes therefore give each symbol one meaning, family by family. Each table lists the object, its symbol and convention, and the course pages that use it; the defining equations follow on their own lines. This is the notes’ own editorial convention. Lecturers’ slides and the papers cited may use other letters, and a page that quotes them translates once.

Graph pages share one set of matrices. The letter P is kept for the random walk alone, so a permutation is \Pi and the GCN propagator is \hat A.

Permutation \Pi, acting on a graph as (\Pi X, \Pi A \Pi^\top) Graph ML ch. 2 · Geometric Data ch. 7

L = D - A, \qquad L_{\mathrm{sym}} = I - D^{-1/2} A D^{-1/2}, \qquad L_{\mathrm{rw}} = I - D^{-1} A

P = D^{-1} A, \qquad \hat A = \tilde D^{-1/2}\, \tilde A\, \tilde D^{-1/2}, \qquad \tilde A = A + I

0 = \lambda_1 \le \lambda_2 \le \dots \le \lambda_n \ \ (\text{eigenvalues of } L), \qquad \mu_1 \ge \mu_2 \ge \dots \ge \mu_n \ \ (\text{eigenvalues of } A)

“Normalized Laplacian” without a qualifier always means L_{\mathrm{sym}}. The matrix I - D^{-1}A is the random-walk Laplacian.

PageRank and personalized PageRank

One parameter, one meaning: \alpha is the teleport (restart) probability, typically 0.15. The probability of following an edge is 1 - \alpha.

Object Symbol and convention Courses that use it (links)
PageRank stationary row vector \pi; teleport probability \alpha Graph ML ch. 0 · Text Mining and NLP ch. 8 (TextRank)
Personalized PageRank from u \pi_u, restarting at the indicator vector e_u Graph ML ch. 2, ch. 7 · Advanced GNN ch. 3
APPNP propagation the same \alpha, propagator \hat A, base predictions H Advanced GNN ch. 3

\pi^\top = (1 - \alpha)\, \pi^\top P + \frac{\alpha}{n}\, \mathbf 1^\top

\pi_u = \alpha \big(I - (1 - \alpha) P^\top\big)^{-1} e_u

Z^{(k+1)} = (1 - \alpha)\, \hat A\, Z^{(k)} + \alpha H

The damping factor of the original PageRank paper and of TextRank is 1 - \alpha. The letter d is not used for it, since d already names a document and a dimension.

Manifolds and curvature

The continuous Laplace–Beltrami operator is negative semi-definite. Its discrete counterpart is a pair of positive semi-definite matrices, and every sign below follows from that choice.

Object Symbol and convention Courses that use it (links)
Laplace–Beltrami operator \Delta_{\mathcal M} = \operatorname{div}_{\mathcal M} \nabla_{\mathcal M}, negative semi-definite Geometric Data ch. 1, ch. 2 · Shapes ch. 5
Discrete operator cotangent stiffness L_c (symmetric, positive semi-definite) and lumped mass matrix M Geometric Data ch. 2 · Shapes ch. 5
Spectrum generalized problem L_c \varphi = \lambda M \varphi, 1-based: 0 = \lambda_1 < \lambda_2 \le \dots Geometric Data ch. 2, ch. 3 · Shapes ch. 8
Heat kernel signature h_t, built on the eigenpairs (\lambda_k, \varphi_k) Geometric Data ch. 3 · Shapes ch. 8
Curvatures mean curvature H, Gaussian curvature K (K means nothing else); outward unit normal \mathbf n Geometric Data ch. 1 · Shapes ch. 7
Mesh and mass the mesh is \mathcal M, the mass matrix is M Geometric Data ch. 1, ch. 2 · Shapes ch. 1

(L_c)_{ij} = -\tfrac12 \big(\cot \alpha_{ij} + \cot \beta_{ij}\big) \ \ (j \sim i), \qquad (L_c)_{ii} = -\sum_{j \sim i} (L_c)_{ij}

L_c\, \varphi_k = \lambda_k\, M\, \varphi_k, \qquad -\Delta_{\mathcal M} \approx M^{-1} L_c

h_t = \sum_{k} e^{-\lambda_k t}\, \varphi_k \varphi_k^\top

\Delta_{\mathcal M}\, \mathbf x = -2H\, \mathbf n

With the outward normal, H > 0 on a sphere. On the unit sphere, \Delta_{\mathcal M} \mathbf x = -2\mathbf x, which checks the sign.

The fraction f_i is normalized by k times the number of tokens, so the fractions sum to one even with top-k routing.

Here b is the batch size, T the number of cached tokens per sequence and s the bytes per element (s = 2 in fp16). For Llama-3-8B in fp16 this gives 128 KiB per token.

Distillation weight \lambda, on the teacher term Advanced DL ch. 3 · CV Applications ch. 8 · Responsible AI ch. 3

\mathrm{softmax}_T(z)_i = \frac{e^{z_i / T}}{\sum_j e^{z_j / T}}

\pi_j \propto \pi_{0,j}\, e^{-\eta\, n \hat R_n(f_j)}, \qquad \pi^\star \propto \pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\, e^{r / \beta}

\mathcal L_{\mathrm{KD}} = (1 - \lambda)\, \ell_{\mathrm{CE}}(z_s, y) + \lambda\, T^2\, \mathrm{KL}\big(\mathrm{softmax}(z_t / T) \,\big\|\, \mathrm{softmax}(z_s / T)\big)

Here z_t and z_s are the teacher and student logits. The factor T^2 keeps the gradient of the soft term on the scale of the hard term. Where \eta or \beta is read as a temperature, the temperature is its inverse.

Probabilistic models and the ELBO

The KL divergence always puts the reference distribution first. The ELBO is a bound that is maximized, so it never borrows the letter of a loss.

Bayesian deep learning each KL bound states its direction Privacy and UQ ch. 8
ELBO \mathrm{ELBO}(\theta, \phi; x), maximized; the EM bound is \mathrm{ELBO}(q, \theta) Deep Learning P1 ch. 9 · Multimodal GenAI ch. 0, ch. 1 · Machine Learning ch. 7
VAE training loss \mathcal L_{\mathrm{VAE}} = -\mathrm{ELBO} Deep Learning P1 ch. 9 · Multimodal GenAI ch. 1

\mathrm{KL}(p \,\|\, q) = \mathbb E_{x \sim p}\Big[\log \frac{p(x)}{q(x)}\Big]

\mathrm{ELBO}(\theta, \phi; x) = \mathbb E_{q_\phi(z \mid x)}\big[\log p_\theta(x \mid z)\big] - \mathrm{KL}\big(q_\phi(z \mid x) \,\|\, p(z)\big) \;\le\; \log p_\theta(x)

\mathcal L_{\mathrm{VAE}}(\theta, \phi; x) = -\,\mathrm{ELBO}(\theta, \phi; x)

The CTC forward–backward follows the same rule: \alpha_t(s) contains the frame-t emission and \beta_t(s) does not. The original CTC paper puts it in both, so its product \alpha_t \beta_t is divided by the emission.

Diffusion

Time runs from data at t = 0 to noise at t = T. The guidance weight w equals 1 for plain conditional sampling.

Object Symbol and convention Courses that use it (links)
Noise schedule \beta_t, \alpha_t = 1 - \beta_t, \bar\alpha_t = \prod_{s \le t} \alpha_s Multimodal GenAI ch. 3 · Deep Learning P2 ch. 7 · Advanced DL ch. 10 · Probability and Monte Carlo ch. 9
Noise predictor \varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t, c); condition c, null condition \varnothing Multimodal GenAI ch. 3, ch. 4 · Control in GenAI ch. 7

Other weights named β \beta_{\mathrm{KL}} for the β-VAE weight, \beta_c for the VQ commitment weight Multimodal GenAI ch. 1

x_t = \sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\, x_0 + \sqrt{1 - \bar\alpha_t}\, \varepsilon, \qquad \varepsilon \sim \mathcal N(0, I)

\nabla_{x_t} \log p_t(x_t) \approx -\frac{\varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t)}{\sqrt{1 - \bar\alpha_t}}

\tilde\varepsilon_w = \varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t, \varnothing) + w \big(\varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t, c) - \varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t, \varnothing)\big)

Some papers write guidance in the form below. Their weight w' is ours minus one, w' = w - 1, so their w' = 0 is plain conditional sampling.

\tilde\varepsilon = (1 + w')\, \varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t, c) - w'\, \varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t, \varnothing)

q(x_t \mid x_{t-1}) = \mathrm{Cat}\big(x_t;\ x_{t-1} Q_t\big), \qquad q(x_t \mid x_0) = \mathrm{Cat}\big(x_t;\ x_0 \bar Q_t\big)

Q_t = \alpha_t I + \beta_t\, \mathbf 1 m^\top \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \bar Q_t = \bar\alpha_t I + (1 - \bar\alpha_t)\, \mathbf 1 m^\top

With marginal transitions toward a law m, as in DiGress, \bar\alpha_t is the probability of keeping the original category. The training loss splits into minimized terms:

-\mathrm{ELBO} = \mathcal L_T + \sum_{t \ge 2} \mathcal L_{t-1} + \mathcal L_0

When a page deviates

Sometimes a page must keep another symbol: it follows a paper line by line, or a letter is already taken in its own chapter. The rule is simple. A page that deviates says so at the start of its notation block, the definition or paragraph where it first fixes its symbols, usually in chapter 0.

That opening line points here and lists only the deviations, each with its translation to the site-wide symbol. The deviation then holds on the whole page: no silent switch halfway through. A course page could open its notation block like this.
