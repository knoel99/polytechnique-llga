Références

  • Décodage contraint : B. Willard, R. Louf, Efficient Guided Generation for LLMs, 2023 (bibliothèque Outlines) ; Y. Dong et al., XGrammar: Flexible and Efficient Structured Generation Engine for LLMs, ICLR 2025 (code) ; T. Scholak, N. Schucher, D. Bahdanau, PICARD, EMNLP 2021.
  • Steering : A. Turner et al., Activation Addition: Steering Language Models Without Optimization, 2023 ; A. Zou et al., Representation Engineering: A Top-Down Approach to AI Transparency, 2023 ; K. Li et al., Inference-Time Intervention, NeurIPS 2023 ; G. Ilharco et al., Editing Models with Task Arithmetic, ICLR 2023.
  • RLHF et alignement : L. Ouyang et al., Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback (InstructGPT), NeurIPS 2022 ; R. Rafailov et al., Direct Preference Optimization, NeurIPS 2023 ; DeepSeek-AI, DeepSeekMath (GRPO), 2024 — le détail pratique est au module LLMs (M2) ch. 5.
  • Watermarking : J. Kirchenbauer et al., A Watermark for Large Language Models, ICML 2023 ; J. Kirchenbauer et al., On the Reliability of Watermarks for LLMs, ICLR 2024 ; X. Zhao et al., Provable Robust Watermarking for AI-Generated Text (unigram), 2023 ; R. Kuditipudi et al., Robust Distortion-free Watermarks for Language Models, TMLR 2024 ; S. Dathathri et al., Scalable watermarking for identifying large language model outputs (SynthID-Text), Nature 634, 2024 ; S. Sadasivan et al., Can AI-Generated Text be Reliably Detected?, 2023 ; M. Christ, S. Gunn, R. Zamir, Undetectable Watermarks for Language Models, 2024 — prolongé par le module Explainability, Security, Privacy ch. 9.
  • Diffusion : guidage et contrôle : J. Ho, T. Salimans, Classifier-Free Diffusion Guidance, 2022 ; L. Zhang, A. Rao, M. Agrawala, Adding Conditional Control to Text-to-Image Diffusion Models (ControlNet), ICCV 2023 (code) ; R. Mokady et al., Null-text Inversion for Editing Real Images, CVPR 2023 ; A. Hertz et al., Prompt-to-Prompt Image Editing with Cross-Attention Control, ICLR 2023 ; C. Meng et al., SDEdit: Guided Image Synthesis and Editing with Stochastic Differential Equations, ICLR 2022 ; T. Brooks et al., InstructPix2Pix, CVPR 2023 — bases au module Multimodal GenAI ch. 3-4.
  • Graphes contrôlés : C. Shi et al., GraphAF: a Flow-based Autoregressive Model for Molecular Graph Generation, ICLR 2020 ; B. Samanta et al., NeVAE: A Deep Generative Model for Molecular Graphs, AAAI 2019 ; C. Vignac et al., DiGress: Discrete Denoising Diffusion for Graph Generation, ICLR 2023 ; J. You et al., GraphRNN, ICML 2018 ; Y. Li et al., Deep Generative Models of Graphs (DeepGMG), ICML 2018 — développés au module Graph Generative Models (M2).
  • Évaluation : T. Kynkäänniemi et al., Improved Precision and Recall Metric for Assessing Generative Models, NeurIPS 2019 ; FID et évaluation des génératrices : module Multimodal GenAI ch. 8 ; évaluation distributionnelle (MMD, FCD) : module Graph Generative Models ch. 6.
  • Cadre réglementaire : Règlement (UE) 2024/1689 — AI Act (art. 50, transparence des contenus générés) ; OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (injections).
