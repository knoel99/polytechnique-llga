Stakes (Extrapolating)

Extrapolated notes. The end-of-studies internship (24 ECTS) is the master’s capstone: broader ownership than in M1, often a professionalizing setting (company, applied lab, startup), and a sustainable deliverable. Empirically, this is where the Graph×LLM track translates into exit skills — see the career profiles of After the master.

Place in the 2-year path

Upstream: all of M1 including the research internship, M2 specialization, ethics seminar, transverse project. Here: long P3 execution. Typical difference vs M1 research internship: more product / team / ops integration, even when the topic stays R&D.

Industry and research links

Industry: productionization, impact measurement, multi-team collaboration. Research: possible PhD path (academic or CIFRE) if the internship produced a solid open question. P1/P2 management and M2 ethics help frame “should we deploy?” not only “can we run it?”.

How to approach it

  • One-sentence goal + measurable success criteria by week 1–2.
  • Explicitly link the topic to 2–3 repo modules (path evidence).
  • Plan a risks / ethics / data section in the report — not a cosmetic appendix.

Extrapolating Framing; internship rules = programme and host organization.
