Stakes (Extrapolating)

Official unit for 2026–2027: INT_52402_EP — 16-week research-oriented internship (academic or in a public/private company), listed in LLGA 1 – S2 and taught in period 3; 20 ECTS per the LLGA wiki. The rest of this page is our own framing. The M1 research internship is the first major test outside the classroom: state a question, read the literature, experiment, and report. Empirically, this is where the Graph×LLM track becomes a topic rather than a pile of modules.

It is not yet the end-of-studies internship (24 ECTS in M2): the accent is research-oriented — academic lab, industrial R&D, or hybrids — with a report / poster / reproducible code deliverable depending on supervision.

Place in the 2-year path

Upstream: P1–P2. Here: P3 immersion. Downstream: M2 specialization then end-of-studies internship. Many M2 topics read better after touching one paper end-to-end in M1.

The career profiles of After the master help decide which direction a topic should serve.

Topic sources. The Year-1 integrative projects (off-curriculum support) each end with an internship seed: one research question, from drug repurposing to statutory-article retrieval. A student who ran one at the end of Period 2 can propose that question to a host lab as a candidate topic, with the project report as preliminary evidence.

Industry and research links

Research: experimental method, baselines, ablations, writing. Industry R&D: same evidence bar with data/prod constraints. Helps choose for M2: doctoral research, ML engineering, or CIFRE frontier.

How to approach it

  • One question, one metric, one baseline — before stacking models.
  • Weekly log: hypothesis → trial → result → next question.
  • Keep an explicit link to at least one repo module (Graph ML, OT, LLM, …).
