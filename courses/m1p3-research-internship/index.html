Stakes (Extrapolating)

Extrapolated notes. The M1 research internship (20 ECTS) is the first major test outside the classroom: state a question, read the literature, experiment, and report. Empirically, this is where the Graph×LLM track becomes a topic rather than a pile of modules.

It is not yet the end-of-studies internship (24 ECTS in M2): the accent is research-oriented — academic lab, industrial R&D, or hybrids — with a report / poster / reproducible code deliverable depending on supervision.

Place in the 2-year path

Upstream: P1–P2. Here: P3 immersion. Downstream: M2 specialization then end-of-studies internship. Many M2 topics read better after touching one paper end-to-end in M1.

See also Exit capabilities for Graph×LLM synthesis questions useful when framing a topic.

Industry and research links

Research: experimental method, baselines, ablations, writing. Industry R&D: same evidence bar with data/prod constraints. Helps choose for M2: doctoral research, ML engineering, or CIFRE frontier.

How to approach it

  • One question, one metric, one baseline — before stacking models.
  • Weekly log: hypothesis → trial → result → next question.
  • Keep an explicit link to at least one repo module (Graph ML, OT, LLM, …).
