Stakes (Extrapolating)

Extrapolated notes. Humanities in a Graph×LLM master’s are not cultural varnish: they train critical reading, argumentation, and situating technology in ideas, power, and representation. Empirically, that is the same muscle as reading a paper without being hypnotized by the leaderboard.

Shared M1/M2 page: both index rows link here. Semester content depends on the current humanities offer.

Place in the 2-year path

M1: during the ML/DL foundation — useful so AI is not reduced to loss minimization. M2: alongside Ethical Issues and Novel Applications of AI and LLM/GNN courses: humanities feed the ethics discussion without replacing it.

Industry and research links

Industry: clear writing, framing ambiguous problems, multi-party trade-offs (legal, product, risk). Research: positioning a contribution, honest literature review, sensitivity to corpus and narrative bias.

How to approach it

  • Tie each humanities theme to a master’s artifact (model, dataset, agent demo).
  • Keep reusable notes for the M2 ethics seminar and internship report.
