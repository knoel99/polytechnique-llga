Références

  • Y. Nesterov, Introductory Lectures on Convex Optimization : A Basic Course, Kluwer, 2004 — la référence du ch. 2–3 (bornes inférieures, accélération).
  • S. Bubeck, Convex Optimization : Algorithms and Complexity, Foundations and Trends in ML, 2015 — le panorama de référence, preuves compactes.
  • A. Beck, First-Order Methods in Optimization, SIAM, 2017 — le traité du proximal (ch. 4).
  • A. Beck, M. Teboulle, A Fast Iterative Shrinkage-Thresholding Algorithm for Linear Inverse Problems (FISTA), SIAM J. Imaging Sciences, 2009.
  • I. Daubechies, M. Defrise, C. De Mol, An Iterative Thresholding Algorithm for Linear Inverse Problems with a Sparsity Constraint (ISTA), Comm. Pure Appl. Math., 2004.
  • H. Robbins, S. Monro, A Stochastic Approximation Method, Annals of Mathematical Statistics, 1951 — l'acte de naissance du SGD.
  • L. Bottou, F. E. Curtis, J. Nocedal, Optimization Methods for Large-Scale Machine Learning, SIAM Review, 2018 — la synthèse moderne des ch. 5–6.
  • R. Johnson, T. Zhang, Accelerating Stochastic Gradient Descent using Predictive Variance Reduction (SVRG), NeurIPS 2013.
  • A. Defazio, F. Bach, S. Lacoste-Julien, SAGA : A Fast Incremental Gradient Method with Support for Non-Strongly Convex Composite Objectives, NeurIPS 2014.
  • B. O'Donoghue, E. Candès, Adaptive Restart for Accelerated Gradient Schemes, Foundations of Computational Mathematics, 2015.
  • J. Nocedal, S. Wright, Numerical Optimization, 2e éd., Springer, 2006 — Newton, BFGS, L-BFGS (ch. 7).
  • D. P. Kingma, J. Ba, Adam : A Method for Stochastic Optimization, ICLR 2015.
  • I. Loshchilov, F. Hutter, Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization (AdamW), ICLR 2019.
  • Z. You, I. Gitman, B. Ginsburg, Large Batch Training of Convolutional Networks (LARS), 2017.
  • J. Bergstra, Y. Bengio, Random Search for Hyper-Parameter Optimization, JMLR, 2012.
  • B. Shahriari et al., Taking the Human Out of the Loop : A Review of Bayesian Optimization, Proceedings of the IEEE, 2016.
  • K. Li et al., Hyperband : A Novel Bandit-Based Approach to Hyperparameter Optimization, ICLR 2017.
  • Équipe pédagogique (CMAP, École Polytechnique) : L. F. O. Chamon, A. Ribeiro, Probably Approximately Correct Constrained Learning, NeurIPS 2020 ; L. F. O. Chamon, S. Paternain, M. Calvo-Fullana, A. Ribeiro, The empirical duality gap of constrained statistical learning, 2020 ; S. Paternain, L. Chamon, M. Calvo-Fullana, A. Ribeiro, Constrained Reinforcement Learning Has Zero Duality Gap, NeurIPS 2019 ; A. Dieuleveut, N. Flammarion, F. Bach, Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger Convergence Rates for Least-Squares Regression, ICML 2017 ; A. Dieuleveut, A. Durmus, F. Bach, Bridging the Gap between Constant Step Size Stochastic Gradient Descent and Markov Chains, Annals of Statistics, 2020.
  • SciPy (L-BFGS, Newton-CG) et scikit-optimize (GP-EI) — les outils des TME.
