Stakes (Extrapolating)

Not in the LLGA 2026–2027 list. In 2026–2027 the Period 2 management units of the track are IME_52441_EP — Simulation d’entreprise, a one-day serious game in which teams of five run competing multinational phone makers (R&D, plant sizing, pricing, accounting, responsible-sourcing trade-offs), and IME_52470_EP — Digital Transformation and AI in Organisations and Society (Daniel Curto), on how AI systems reshape organisations, work and decision-making once they leave the lab (group project and individual exam).

The startup/entrepreneurship pair below comes from the earlier wiki curriculum and is kept as background. Extrapolated notes. Brochure title: Startup and Large Companies: Building a Bridge for Innovation or Entrepreneurship for Sustainability / Case Studies on Innovation. Shared thread: how an innovation (often technical) crosses the startup ↔︎ large-company boundary under governance, risk, and sometimes sustainability constraints.

After P1 strategy/marketing, P2 pushes to cases: alliances, scaling, and friction between startup speed and corporate process — very visible when industrializing an AI model.

Place in the 2-year path

Upstream: Strategy / Marketing. Here (P2): applied innovation during the RL / Graph ML / NLP core. Downstream: P3 research internship, then M2 transverse project + final internship.

Industry and research links

Industry: intrapreneurship, corporate venture, tech transfer, AI product ops. Research: result maturity (TRL), IP, open-source vs proprietary — recurring in lab internships and CIFRE.

How to approach it

  • Take a P2 technical deliverable (e.g. graph+text demo) and write the startup/corporate “bridge”: who pays, who operates, what risk.
  • If the sustainability option is chosen, cross-link with responsible / optimization modules.

Extrapolating Framing support; cases and grading stay with official teaching.
