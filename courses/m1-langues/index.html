Stakes (Extrapolating)

Extrapolated notes. In a mostly English-facing master’s (course titles, papers, stacks), languages are not a “TOEIC bonus”: they are the ability to produce — papers, GitHub issues, design reviews, defenses — and to collaborate in mixed FR/EN teams (or another L2/L3).

Shared page for both M1 and M2 index rows.

Place in the 2-year path

M1: secure written/oral production during early reports and orals. M1 · P3 / M2: scientific English for the research internship, LLM/GNN readings, then an international or bilingual final internship. This repo’s FR/EN mirrors exist for the same reason.

Industry and research links

Industry: technical docs, RFCs, incident calls, international clients. Research: abstracts, peer review, conferences; French remains useful for national reports / CIFRE / doctoral colleges.

How to approach it

  • Reuse course vocabulary (attention, message-passing, calibration) in short target-language talks.
  • Keep a personal FR↔︎EN glossary of track terms.
