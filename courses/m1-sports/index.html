Stakes (Extrapolating)

Extrapolated notes — not a sports manual. Sport at École Polytechnique is not decorative: it is schedule discipline and habits (consistency, load management, teamwork) that empirically support surviving a dense master’s (courses + projects + internships).

The curriculum wiki lists sports twice: among the mandatory non-scientific units of M1 · Period 1, and again in the M2 transverse block, where humanities, languages and sports share 8 ECTS. One shared framing page is enough; concrete activities depend on the semester’s sport offer.

Place in the 2-year path

M1: from P1 during the ML/DL ramp-up. M2: continuity through LLM / Graph AI specialization and the transverse project. A counterweight to exam and deliverable crunch weeks.

Transferable skills

No direct “AI research” link — rather stamina skills: prioritization, recovery, teamwork, stress management. In research or final internships, sustainable pace matters as much as a benchmark.

How to approach it

  • Treat slots as non-negotiable in revision / project planning.
  • Connect physical load and cognitive load: same recovery logic.
  • Put the slots on the same calendar as the track’s team projects. The six-week Year 1 project at the end of Period 2 and the build weeks of the Year 2 transverse project are the weeks when sport is easiest to drop.
  • Carry the team-sport habits over to those projects: clear roles, showing up, a short debrief after each match or milestone. This is the teamwork side of objective O7.
